# Module 8: Background & Composition Designer

**Purpose:** Design the background treatment and composition rules for each shot — feeding directly into Module 7's prompts, and critical for platform compliance (Module 10) since many marketplaces have strict background requirements for primary listing images.

## Process

1. **Determine background treatment per shot type**: pure white/transparent for marketplace main images (per most platforms' requirements), a contextual/lifestyle background for secondary or social images (consistent with Module 5's style), or a neutral studio background for detail shots.
2. **Apply composition principles per shot**: rule-of-thirds placement for lifestyle shots, centered/symmetrical framing for clean product-only shots, adequate negative space if the image needs to accommodate text overlay (for ads or social posts specifically).
3. **Specify camera angle per shot** — a 3/4 angle often shows a product's dimensionality best for a hero shot; a straight-on angle often suits detail/macro shots; an overhead angle suits flat-lay compositions.
4. **Flag any composition requirement specific to a platform** (feeds Module 10 in fuller detail) — e.g. a marketplace requiring the product fill a minimum percentage of the frame.

## Output format

```
PER-SHOT BACKGROUND & COMPOSITION:
[Shot] — Background: [white/transparent/lifestyle/studio] — Composition: [rule-of-thirds/centered/etc.] — Angle: [3/4, straight-on, overhead]
(repeat per shot)
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 7's prompts directly, and Module 10's platform-compliance check.
