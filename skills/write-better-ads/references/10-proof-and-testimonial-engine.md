# Module 10: Proof & Testimonial Engine

**Purpose:** Turn raw results and testimonials into ad-ready proof creative — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`, with an emphasis on the UGC (user-generated-content) style that performs especially well in ad formats specifically.

## Process

1. **Categorize available proof**: results/case studies, social proof (volume), authority proof, peer/UGC-style proof.
2. **Extract the specific outcome** from each raw testimonial without inventing anything.
3. **Prioritize UGC-style proof for ad creative specifically** — a genuine, slightly imperfect customer testimonial (video or screenshot) often outperforms polished, branded proof in ad placements, since it reads as more authentic and less like traditional advertising.
4. **Match proof to the social-proof-led angle from Module 5** directly, and note where proof can be woven briefly into other angles as supporting color.

## Output format

```
PROOF BLOCKS:
[Type: Result/Social/Authority/UGC-Peer]
Ad-ready version: "[content-ready for the social-proof angle's ad format]"
(repeat per proof piece)

GAPS: [what's missing — flag for the user to go collect, especially UGC-style proof if only polished testimonials exist]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 5's social-proof angle directly, and Module 8/9 for placement in copy and creative.
