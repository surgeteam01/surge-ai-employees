# Module 12: Breakup & Re-Engagement Writer

**Purpose:** Write the final touch in the sequence — the honest "closing the loop" message for prospects who haven't responded — and the path for re-engaging them later rather than losing them permanently.

## Process

1. **Write the breakup touch as genuinely low-pressure**, not a guilt trip or a fake-urgency last chance. The goal is to close the loop respectfully: acknowledge the lack of response, restate the core value in one line, and make clear this is the last touch in this specific sequence.
2. **Leave the door open honestly** — many replies actually come from the breakup touch, precisely because it removes pressure rather than adding it. A line like "I'll leave it here for now — if it's ever relevant, just reply and I'll pick this back up" outperforms anything that manufactures scarcity a stranger hasn't earned the right to invoke.
3. **Never fabricate urgency or scarcity in this touch** (a fake "closing your file" deadline, a fake limited-time offer) — this is a trust-building message, not a sales close; false pressure here is more likely to trigger a spam complaint than a reply.
4. **Define the re-engagement path** — what happens to a non-responder after the breakup touch: moved to a long-term, low-frequency nurture list (e.g. an occasional value-add touch every 1-2 months, not the active sequence's cadence), removed entirely if they explicitly opted out, or flagged for a different angle/offer in a future campaign.

## Output format

```
BREAKUP TOUCH (Channel: [x])
Body: [full message copy — low-pressure, door left open]

RE-ENGAGEMENT PATH:
Non-responders → [long-term nurture cadence / removed / flagged for future campaign]
Explicit opt-outs → [removed immediately, per compliance constraints in Module 1]
```

## Handoff

Feeds into Module 13 as the sequence's final scheduled touch. The re-engagement path defined here should be respected by whoever manages the list going forward — a breakup touch that doesn't specify what happens next just lets prospects fall through the cracks.
