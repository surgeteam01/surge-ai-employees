# Module 14: Outreach Optimizer

**Purpose:** Score the finished sequence, improve reply-rate potential and compliance, and produce A/B variants — the final quality gate before the sequence goes out to real prospects.

## Process

1. **Score the assembled sequence** on a simple rubric, 1-5 each:
   - **Personalization depth** — does each touch actually use the signals confirmed as real in Module 4, or does it read as generic despite claiming relevance?
   - **Brevity** — is every touch short enough to read in the time a busy prospect actually gives a cold message?
   - **Value-to-ask ratio** — do the middle touches add real value, or does the sequence feel like repeated asking?
   - **Reply-worthiness** — does each touch make replying easy and natural, or does it read as a dead-end broadcast?
   - **Proof credibility** — is proof specific and segment-matched, or generic?
   - **Sequence coherence** — do the touches read as one continuous outreach, or as disconnected messages?
2. **Run a compliance check**: working opt-out mechanism present on every cold email (per CAN-SPAM/GDPR as applicable from Module 1's constraints), accurate sender identification, no misleading subject lines, and confirmation that LinkedIn touches respect that platform's connection/messaging limits and terms.
3. **Flag the lowest-scoring 1-2 dimensions** and rewrite those touches rather than doing a generic full-sequence polish — targeted fixes beat diffuse ones.
4. **Check deliverability basics for the email touches**: spam-trigger words and patterns, a reasonable text-to-link ratio, and whether subject lines look like genuine one-to-one email rather than a broadcast blast.
5. **Produce at least one full A/B variant** — typically an alternate opening line/hook for Touch 1 and an alternate CTA framing, since Touch 1's hook has the highest leverage-per-word-changed of anything in the sequence (it decides whether the rest gets read at all). Note what hypothesis each variant tests.

## Output format

```
SCORES (1-5): Personalization Depth:_ Brevity:_ Value-to-Ask Ratio:_ Reply-Worthiness:_ Proof Credibility:_ Sequence Coherence:_

COMPLIANCE CHECK: [pass / issues found — list specific touches and fixes]

TOP FIXES MADE: [list, tied to lowest scores]

A/B VARIANT (on Touch 1):
- Opening line: [alternate]
- CTA: [alternate]
- Hypothesis being tested: [one line]
```

## Handoff

This is the last stage — present the final scored sequence, the objection reply playbook, and the A/B variant to the user as the finished deliverable, ready to load into their outreach tool or hand to a sales rep. If any score is 3 or below, say so plainly rather than presenting the sequence as "done" — flag it as a known weak point and what it would take to fix (e.g. "Personalization depth scored low because no research tooling is available at this list size — recommend narrowing the list or accepting segment-level personalization only").
