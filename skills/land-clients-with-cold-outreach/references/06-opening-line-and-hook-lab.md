# Module 6: Opening Line & Hook Lab

**Purpose:** Write and score the first line of the first touch — in cold outreach this single line does more work than a headline does on a sales page, since it's the entire difference between a reply and an instant delete or "not interested."

## Process

1. **Generate at least 5-8 opening line options**, drawing on the sharpened offer (Module 2), the top pain/desire (Module 3), and the personalization signals confirmed as realistic (Module 4) — never invent a personalization detail that wasn't actually gathered. Use multiple formulas so options are genuinely different:
   - **Trigger-based**: opens around a real, specific event or detail about the prospect/company — the strongest option when true 1:1 research is available.
   - **Pain-first, no pitch**: names the specific pain in the prospect's own language (from the exact-phrase bank) without mentioning the offer yet.
   - **Pattern interrupt**: breaks the expected "hope you're doing well" cold-email cadence with something direct or unexpected — effective but riskier; must still feel relevant, not gimmicky.
   - **Peer-reference**: references a comparable company/role the business has helped, without overclaiming ("working with a few teams like yours on...").
   - **Direct and short**: states the reason for reaching out plainly in one sentence — works well for senior/busy ICPs who read fast and skim ruthlessly.
2. **Score each option** on: Relevance (does it prove this wasn't copy-pasted to a thousand people?), Believability (does the personalization/claim hold up to scrutiny?), Brevity (cold opens die in dense paragraphs — shorter almost always wins), and Reply-worthiness (does it invite a response, or does it read as a dead-end statement?).
3. **Pick the top option per channel** (email opens can run slightly longer than a LinkedIn DM or connection note, which needs to be shorter still) and note why it wins.

## Output format

```
OPENING LINE OPTIONS (scored 1-5 on Relevance / Believability / Brevity / Reply-worthiness):
1. [line] — R:_ B:_ Br:_ RW:_
2. ...

TOP PICK (Email): [line]
TOP PICK (LinkedIn, if in scope): [line — shorter variant]
TOP PICK (Cold call opener, if in scope): [line — spoken variant]
Why these win: [one or two sentences each]
```

## Handoff

The top pick's language and personalization angle should carry directly into Module 7's first touch — the opening line isn't a separate artifact, it's the first sentence of that touch. Module 10 (Multi-Channel Adapter) uses the channel-specific variants noted here rather than re-deriving them.
