# Module 8: Proof & Testimonial Engine

**Purpose:** Turn raw results, case studies, and testimonials into short, credible proof lines that fit inside a cold message without turning it into a sales page. Vague praise does almost nothing in outreach — specific, outcome-focused proof is what makes a stranger's claim believable.

## Process

1. **Categorize available proof** into types: results/case studies (numbers, before-after, ideally from a similar company/role to the prospect), social proof (volume or notable names — "we've worked with 40+ teams like yours"), authority proof (credentials, media, notable clients), and peer proof (a testimonial from someone in a comparable role).
2. **For each raw testimonial or result, extract the specific outcome** and compress it to one line short enough to sit inside a 3-5 sentence cold message. Never fabricate a result, number, or quote that wasn't given. If a testimonial is vague, either ask the user for the specific result behind it or hold it back rather than using it as a headline claim.
3. **Match proof to segment**, not just to a generic library — a case study from a similar company/industry to the prospect (per Module 4's segments) lands far harder than a generic "we help businesses grow" line.
4. **Prioritize the single strongest, most relevant proof point per segment** rather than listing several — cold messages have room for one credible proof line, not a portfolio.

## Output format

```
PROOF LINES BY SEGMENT:

[Segment/Industry]
One-line proof: "[specific, compressed result]"
Full version (for a reply/call, not the initial touch): "[2-3 sentence version]"

(repeat per segment)

GAPS: [segments with no matching proof — flag for the user to go collect, or recommend using the closest available proof and saying so plainly if asked]
```

## Handoff

If "none yet" was the answer in Module 1, say so plainly here rather than inventing placeholder results — recommend leading with a founder-credibility angle or a specific process/methodology claim instead until real case studies exist. Proof lines here get dropped into Module 7's touches at the placements that module calls for, and referenced (not repeated) inside the objection playbook in Module 9.
