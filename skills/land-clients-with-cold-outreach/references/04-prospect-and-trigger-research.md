# Module 4: Prospect & Trigger Research

**Purpose:** Find the per-prospect or per-segment signals that make a cold message feel researched rather than blasted — this is the single biggest lever in cold outreach reply rates, and it doesn't exist as a concept in a sales page or an email funnel, since both of those address one audience at once rather than one prospect at a time.

## Process

1. **Distinguish two levels of personalization** and decide which is realistic given the list size (from Module 1): true 1:1 research (reading a specific prospect's recent posts, news about their specific company, a specific trigger event) works at low volume (tens to low hundreds); segment-level personalization (a variable swapped in based on industry, role, or company size) works at higher volume. Don't promise 1:1 depth against a 10,000-prospect list — say so plainly and recommend segment-level instead.
2. **Identify trigger events** worth opening a message around: funding announcements, leadership changes, job postings that reveal a gap (e.g. hiring for a role the offer replaces or supports), company expansion or contraction news, a recent public complaint or ask (social media, forum, review), a relevant industry event or report. Note which triggers are realistically detectable given the user's available tools (manual LinkedIn research vs. a scraping/enrichment tool vs. none).
3. **Build a personalization variable list** for segment-level outreach: what changes per segment (industry-specific pain phrasing, role-specific outcome framing, company-size-specific proof point) so Module 7 can template the sequence without it reading as a mail-merge.
4. **Flag what's not realistic.** If the user has no research tools and a large cold list, say plainly that deep personalization isn't feasible at that volume and recommend either narrowing the list or accepting lighter, segment-level personalization — overpromising research depth here sets up messages that can't actually be written as described.

## Output format

```
PERSONALIZATION LEVEL: [1:1 / segment-level] — reason: [list size + available tools]

TRIGGER EVENTS TO WATCH FOR: [list, tied to what's realistically detectable]

PERSONALIZATION VARIABLES (for segment-level):
- [Segment/role] → [pain phrasing / proof point / angle to use]
(repeat per segment)

FEASIBILITY FLAG: [any gap between desired personalization depth and what's realistic at this list size/tooling]
```

## Handoff

The personalization level and variables here directly shape Module 6 (opening lines must actually be writable using only the signals confirmed here) and Module 7 (the sequence should reference these variables/triggers, not invent research that wasn't actually gathered).
