# Module 2: Offer Clarifier

**Purpose:** Sharpen the offer and the transformation it delivers before a single message is written. Most cold outreach fails here, not at the opening line — a vague offer makes every message that follows sound like generic prospecting, and generic prospecting gets deleted.

## Process

1. **State the current offer in one sentence**, as the user described it.
2. **Stress-test it against the Value Equation**: Dream Outcome (how big/specific is the result?), Likelihood of Success (how believable is it, especially from a stranger's cold message?), Time Delay (how fast do they see results?), Effort & Sacrifice (how easy is it?). Flag whichever of these four is weakest or missing.
3. **Rewrite the offer as a before → after transformation**, not a feature list. Bad: "we do SEO consulting." Good: "go from page 3 on Google for your top keywords to page 1 within a quarter, without hiring an in-house team."
4. **Check specificity.** Vague promises ("grow your business," "improve your results") are exactly what makes cold outreach sound like every other cold message a prospect has ever ignored. Push for numbers, timeframes, and named outcomes wherever honestly possible.
5. **Flag anything unprovable or overclaimed** — a stranger's cold message has less earned trust than a warm audience's, so an inflated promise here is even more likely to trigger skepticism or a spam report. It will also surface again at Module 9 (Objection Handler) as a credibility problem.

## Output format

```
CURRENT OFFER (as given): [one sentence]
WEAKEST LINK: [Dream Outcome / Likelihood / Time / Effort]
SHARPENED OFFER (one sentence): [rewrite]
TRANSFORMATION STATEMENT (one paragraph, before → after): [rewrite]
RED FLAGS: [anything overclaimed, vague, or unprovable]
```

## Handoff

The sharpened offer and transformation statement become the backbone of Module 5 (Outreach Architect), Module 6 (Opening Line & Hook Lab), and Module 7 (Message Sequence). If the user pushes back on the sharpened version, don't just default back to their original wording — ask which specific part felt wrong and refine, since a vague offer will weaken every touch in the sequence, not just one message.
