# Module 3: Market Landscape Scan

**Purpose:** Understand who else already serves this general space, and where the gaps are — a niche chosen without reference to the existing landscape risks either picking an already-saturated angle or missing a genuinely open opportunity nearby.

## Process

1. **Identify existing players** in the industry/area of interest from Module 1: direct competitors, adjacent players serving a similar audience with a different angle, and any well-known "big name" positioning in the space that smaller players have to differentiate against.
2. **Categorize how existing players position themselves** — by audience segment (who they serve), by mechanism/method (how they deliver results), by price point, or by format (course vs. done-for-you vs. community). This reveals which positioning axes are already crowded and which are open.
3. **Identify gaps** — an underserved segment (a specific audience niche within the broader space that existing players ignore or serve poorly), an underused mechanism (a genuinely different way of delivering the same underlying result), or a positioning combination nobody else has claimed (e.g. a specific audience + a specific mechanism together).
4. **Flag genuinely saturated angles** honestly — if a specific segment/mechanism combination is already dominated by well-established players with strong proof, say so, rather than recommending the user compete head-on without a real differentiation plan (that's Module 8's job, but the flag belongs here).

## Output format

```
EXISTING PLAYERS: [list, with how each positions itself — segment/mechanism/price/format]

CROWDED POSITIONING AXES: [where most players cluster]

GAPS IDENTIFIED:
- [Underserved segment / underused mechanism / open combination] — why it looks open: [reasoning]
(repeat per gap)

SATURATED ANGLES TO FLAG: [any segment/mechanism combination that's already dominated, with strong incumbents]
```

## Handoff

Gaps identified here directly inform Module 4's candidate segment generation — candidate segments shouldn't be invented in a vacuum, they should be checked against what this scan found. Module 8 (Differentiation) references the crowded axes and saturated angles here when deciding how the eventual niche needs to stand apart.
