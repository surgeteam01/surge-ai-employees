# Module 8: Differentiation & Unique Mechanism Finder

**Purpose:** Identify what makes this specific angle distinct from other people already serving similar audiences — a well-chosen segment still needs a reason to be chosen over the alternatives a prospect could find instead.

## Process

1. **Revisit Module 3's crowded positioning axes and existing players** — differentiation needs to be real relative to who's actually out there, not invented in a vacuum.
2. **Identify a genuine unique mechanism** — a specific method, framework, sequence, or point of view that explains *why* this approach gets the result, distinct from "we just care more" or other non-differentiators. A named, describable mechanism (a specific 3-step process, a particular philosophy that runs counter to conventional approaches in the space) is far stronger than an unspecific claim of quality or effort.
3. **Ground the mechanism in Module 2's real experience/results** — a fabricated-sounding mechanism invented purely for marketing purposes tends to read as hollow; the strongest mechanisms come from genuinely how the person actually gets results, described clearly enough to be repeatable and explainable.
4. **Check the differentiation against the segment's actual decision criteria** (if surfaced in Module 5's research) — differentiation that doesn't map to something the segment actually cares about when choosing between options isn't functionally differentiating, even if it's technically true and unique.

## Output format

```
UNIQUE MECHANISM: [name/description of the specific method, framework, or point of view]
WHY IT'S GENUINELY DIFFERENT: [relative to Module 3's existing players and crowded axes]
GROUNDED IN: [which real experience/result from Module 2 this comes from]
MAPS TO SEGMENT'S DECISION CRITERIA: [yes/how, or flag if this needs rethinking]
```

## Handoff

This mechanism becomes part of Module 7's expanded positioning variants (if not already folded in) and Module 12's messaging — it's often the single most quotable, memorable element of the eventual bio/pitch.
