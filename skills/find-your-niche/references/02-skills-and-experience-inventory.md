# Module 2: Skills & Experience Inventory

**Purpose:** Build a real, specific inventory of expertise, results, and credibility — this is the raw material every candidate niche in this pipeline gets built from. A niche invented without reference to genuine expertise is a positioning exercise disconnected from what the person can actually deliver and credibly claim.

## Process

1. **List every skill, credential, and area of real experience**, pushing past vague self-description ("I'm good with people") toward specifics ("I ran client onboarding for a 40-person agency for 3 years and cut time-to-first-value from 6 weeks to 10 days").
2. **List concrete results achieved** — for the person's own work, for past employers, for past clients, or for themselves personally (e.g. a personal transformation that could become the basis of a coaching niche). Specific, measurable results are far stronger niche foundations than general skill claims.
3. **Identify unfair advantages** — anything the person has that most competitors in an adjacent space wouldn't: a rare combination of two skills, an insider perspective from having been on the other side (e.g. a former buyer now selling to buyers), a personal story that builds unusual trust with a specific audience, or access to a community/network others don't have.
4. **Identify gaps honestly** — areas where the person has interest but not yet real experience or results; these aren't disqualifying, but they should be labeled clearly as "aspirational" rather than blended in with proven expertise, since Module 6's viability scoring needs to weight them differently.

## Output format

```
SKILLS & CREDENTIALS: [specific list, not vague self-description]

CONCRETE RESULTS: [specific, measurable results — for self, employers, clients, or personal transformation]

UNFAIR ADVANTAGES: [rare combinations, insider perspective, personal story, network/access]

ASPIRATIONAL AREAS (interest without proven experience yet): [list, clearly labeled]
```

## Handoff

This inventory is the foundation Module 4 draws candidate segments from (a segment should connect to at least one real skill/result/advantage here, not be invented independently) and Module 6 uses to score personal fit for each candidate niche.
