# Module 8: Iterative Refinement & Follow-Up Technique

**Purpose:** Teach how to improve an AI response through targeted follow-up rather than restarting from scratch each time — one of the most underused techniques, since many users abandon a close-but-not-quite response instead of refining it.

## Process

1. **Teach giving specific, actionable feedback on what to change** — "make it better" produces another generic pass; "the second paragraph is too long and the tone is too formal — tighten it and make it more conversational" gives the AI something concrete to act on.
2. **Teach pointing at specific parts of the response** rather than commenting on the whole thing — referencing "the opening line" or "the third bullet point" focuses the revision precisely instead of risking the AI changing things that were already right.
3. **Teach when to iterate vs. when to restart** — if the response is fundamentally off-track (wrong approach entirely, not just needing adjustment), a fresh, more specific initial prompt (informed by Modules 3-7) is often faster than trying to iteratively patch a response built on the wrong foundation.
4. **Teach preserving what's working** — explicitly confirming what to keep unchanged when asking for a revision, so the next pass doesn't accidentally lose good elements while fixing the flagged issue.

## Output format

```
SPECIFIC FEEDBACK TECHNIQUE: [example of vague vs. specific follow-up]
POINTING AT SPECIFIC PARTS: [example]
ITERATE VS. RESTART: [how to tell which applies]
PRESERVING WHAT WORKS: [technique for confirming what to keep]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 14 — the live rewrite demonstration should show not just an improved initial prompt but, if relevant, an example of a good follow-up refinement too.
