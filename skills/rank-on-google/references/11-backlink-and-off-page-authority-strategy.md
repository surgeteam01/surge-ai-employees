# Module 11: Backlink & Off-Page Authority Strategy

**Purpose:** Design realistic tactics for earning links from other sites — a major ranking factor outside the page's own content, and one that requires genuine outreach or genuinely link-worthy content rather than shortcuts, which search engines actively penalize.

## Process

1. **Identify genuinely link-worthy content opportunities** — original research/data, a comprehensive resource that other sites in the niche would want to reference, or a genuinely useful free tool/template — content built specifically to attract citations, not just to rank on its own.
2. **Design a realistic outreach approach**: guest posting on relevant, genuinely reputable sites in the niche (quality over volume — a single link from a genuinely authoritative, relevant site outweighs many low-quality ones), digital PR (pitching a genuinely newsworthy angle, data point, or expert commentary to journalists/bloggers), and resource-page outreach (finding existing "resource" or "further reading" pages in the niche and pitching genuinely relevant additions).
3. **Never recommend manipulative link schemes** — paid links without disclosure, link farms, or artificial link networks are against search engine guidelines and carry real penalty risk; if the user asks about these, explain the risk directly rather than providing tactics for it.
4. **Set realistic expectations and timeline** — backlink building is typically slow and compounding, not something producing results in weeks; flag this honestly rather than implying quick wins.

## Output format

```
LINK-WORTHY CONTENT OPPORTUNITIES: [original research/data, comprehensive resource, free tool/template ideas]

OUTREACH APPROACH:
Guest posting targets: [type of relevant, reputable sites to pursue]
Digital PR angle: [genuinely newsworthy angle/data/expert commentary]
Resource-page outreach: [where to find and how to pitch]

REALISTIC TIMELINE: [set expectations — this is slow, compounding work]
```
