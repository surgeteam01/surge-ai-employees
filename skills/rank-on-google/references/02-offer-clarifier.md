# Module 2: Offer Clarifier

**Purpose:** Sharpen the underlying business goal behind the ranking effort — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`'s Offer Clarifier logic. Ranking is never the actual goal; it's a means to traffic that converts into something (leads, sales, authority), and losing sight of that produces content optimized for search engines but not for the business.

## Process

1. **State the current goal in one sentence** — what should ranking actually produce for the business?
2. **Stress-test against the Value Equation** as it applies to the eventual visitor's experience once they land on the page.
3. **Rewrite as a before → after transformation** for the searcher, not just a ranking target — "rank #1 for X" isn't a real goal on its own; "become the page that a searcher trying to solve X finds, trusts, and converts on" is.
4. **Check specificity** of the target topic — an overly broad topic is both harder to rank for and harder to fully satisfy search intent on.
5. **Flag anything unprovable or overclaimed.**

## Output format

```
CURRENT GOAL (as given): [one sentence]
SHARPENED GOAL (searcher-outcome framed): [rewrite]
TRANSFORMATION STATEMENT (searcher's before → after): [rewrite]
RED FLAGS: [anything overclaimed, vague, or unprovable]
```
