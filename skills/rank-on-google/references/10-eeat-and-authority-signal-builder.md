# Module 10: E-E-A-T & Authority Signal Builder

**Purpose:** Build genuine Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness signals into each page — Google's own quality guidelines weight these heavily, particularly for topics where inaccurate information could cause real harm (health, finance, safety), but they matter to some degree for every page competing against established, authoritative competitors.

## Process

1. **Identify genuine experience signals** — first-hand experience with the topic (a specific result, a specific project, a specific mistake learned from) woven into the content itself, not just claimed in an author bio. Never fabricate experience or credentials that don't exist — this is both an honesty requirement and a real risk, since fabricated expertise is exactly what quality-focused search algorithms and human quality raters are designed to catch.
2. **Identify genuine expertise signals** — specific, accurate technical detail that a surface-level or AI-generated-without-real-knowledge piece of content typically lacks; recommend the user's real domain knowledge be reflected directly in the content rather than generic industry-standard statements.
3. **Build authoritativeness signals**: a clear, credible author byline with real credentials, citations/references to authoritative sources where relevant, and original data, research, or case studies if available (which also naturally attracts backlinks per Module 11).
4. **Build trustworthiness signals**: accurate, honest claims (consistent with Module 2's red-flags check), clear contact/about information on the site, and, for any transactional content, transparent pricing/policy information.

## Output format

```
EXPERIENCE SIGNALS: [specific first-hand detail woven into the page — never fabricated]
EXPERTISE SIGNALS: [specific technical accuracy/depth reflecting real domain knowledge]
AUTHORITATIVENESS SIGNALS: [author byline/credentials, citations, original data if available]
TRUSTWORTHINESS SIGNALS: [accurate claims, contact/about clarity, transparent policies where relevant]
```

## Handoff

These signals should be woven directly into Module 8's content (not bolted on as a separate section) and feed Module 13's assembly.
