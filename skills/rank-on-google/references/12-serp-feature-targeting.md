# Module 12: SERP Feature Targeting

**Purpose:** Structure content specifically to win featured snippets, "People Also Ask" boxes, and other rich SERP features — these appear above standard organic results and can capture significant traffic even from a lower organic ranking position.

## Process

1. **Identify featured-snippet opportunities** from Module 3's question bank — snippets are most commonly won by content that answers a specific question directly and concisely near the top of a well-structured section, often in a short paragraph, a numbered list, or a table, immediately following a heading that closely matches the question's phrasing.
2. **Structure specific sections to directly answer specific questions** — a clear, self-contained 40-60 word answer immediately under a heading phrased as the question itself, followed by more detailed elaboration afterward for readers who want more.
3. **Identify "People Also Ask" opportunities** — related questions from Module 3's research that could each become their own clearly-headed subsection, increasing the chance of appearing in that SERP feature for multiple related queries from a single page.
4. **Identify structured-data opportunities** where relevant (recipe, review, FAQ, how-to schema) if the CMS/platform (Module 1) supports implementing structured data markup — flagged as an implementation recommendation.

## Output format

```
FEATURED SNIPPET TARGETS:
[Question] → Section heading: [question-phrased] → Direct answer (40-60 words): [...]
(repeat per snippet opportunity)

PEOPLE ALSO ASK TARGETS: [related questions to structure as their own subsections]

STRUCTURED DATA OPPORTUNITIES: [schema type, if platform supports it]
```

## Handoff

These specific answer blocks should be woven into Module 8's content at the corresponding headings, not treated as separate content.
