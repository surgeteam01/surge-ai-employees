# Module 10: Objection Handler

**Purpose:** Surface the real objections a buyer will have about a fixed-scope package specifically — productized services face a distinct objection profile from a one-off digital product, centered on fit and flexibility rather than just price.

## Process

1. **Start from Module 3's objection list**, then add the objections specific to productization: "what if my situation doesn't fit the standard package," "can you customize this for my specific case," "why is this more rigid than a custom quote would be," "how do I know this will actually cover what I need," and standard price/trust objections.
2. **Write a direct rebuttal for each** — the fit/flexibility objections in particular should be answered by pointing to the clarity of Module 4's scope boundary and the priced add-on path, not by quietly implying flexibility that isn't real. Overpromising customization here just re-creates the bespoke-scoping problem this skill exists to eliminate.
3. **Handle the "why is this more rigid" framing directly and honestly** — the rebuttal isn't to deny the rigidity, it's to reframe it as the source of the predictability, speed, and price benefit the client is actually getting; rigidity and value are the trade the client is making, and the rebuttal should own that trade rather than dodge it.
4. **Write the FAQ in the client's actual phrasing** (from Module 3's phrase bank).

## Output format

```
OBJECTIONS HANDLED:
1. [Objection] → [Rebuttal] → context: [when this typically comes up]
2. ...

FAQ (client-phrased):
Q: [question in client's actual words]
A: [direct, honest answer]
(repeat)
```

## Handoff

These rebuttals feed Module 12's presentation copy (the strongest 1-2 should be pre-empted directly on the package page) and should be handed to the user as a ready reference for sales conversations, alongside the guarantee from Module 11.
