# Module 9: Proof & Testimonial Engine

**Purpose:** Turn raw results and rough testimonials into persuasive, specific proof blocks matched to specific packages — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`, since the underlying work (extracting a specific outcome, matching proof to the right moment) is identical for a service package.

## Process

1. **Categorize available proof** into types: results/case studies (numbers, before-after), social proof (volume — "we've delivered this for 60+ clients"), authority proof (credentials, media, notable clients), and peer proof (testimonials from clients similar to the target audience).
2. **For each raw testimonial, extract the specific outcome** buried inside it. Rewrite for clarity and impact without inventing anything — never fabricate a result, number, or quote that wasn't given.
3. **Match proof to tier** where possible — a case study from a client who bought the premium tier is stronger proof for that tier specifically than generic proof applied uniformly across all three.
4. **Write proof in more than one length**: a one-line pull-quote and a fuller 2-4 sentence version, so Module 12 can choose based on placement.

## Output format

```
PROOF BLOCKS:

[Type: Result/Authority/Social/Peer]
Short: "[one-line version]"
Full: "[2-4 sentence version]"
Best placement: [which tier, or "general — applies across tiers"]

(repeat per proof piece)

GAPS: [what proof is missing — flag for the user to go collect, especially per-tier proof if only one tier has case studies so far]
```

## Handoff

If "none yet" was the answer in Module 1, say so plainly here rather than inventing placeholder testimonials — recommend leading with a founder-credibility angle or an introductory-pricing strategy for the first few productized clients instead. Proof placements feed directly into Module 12.
