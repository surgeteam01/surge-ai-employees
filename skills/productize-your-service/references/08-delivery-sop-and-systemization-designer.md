# Module 8: Delivery SOP & Systemization Designer

**Purpose:** Build the repeatable internal process that actually delivers the fixed scope consistently, on time, at the assumed cost — this is the module with no equivalent anywhere else in this repo, and arguably the single most important one in this skill: a package can be scoped and priced perfectly and still fail to be "productized" if delivery still happens ad hoc, differently, from scratch every time.

## Process

1. **Map the delivery process as a repeatable sequence of steps**, from the moment a client buys through final delivery: intake (what information/access is needed from the client, and how it's collected — a standard intake form beats an ad hoc kickoff call for consistency), production (the actual work steps, in order), quality check, and delivery/handoff.
2. **Identify what can be templated or automated** at each step — intake forms, standard project folders/templates, checklists, boilerplate sections of the deliverable that don't change client to client, and any tooling that removes manual repetition. The more of the process that's templated, the more the "custom feel, productized cost" gap closes.
3. **Build a checklist version of the process** — a step-by-step internal SOP a team member (including a future hire) could follow to deliver the exact scope from Module 4, without needing the original service provider's judgment call on each project. If the process still depends entirely on one person's tacit expertise at every step, flag this as a scaling risk, not a finished SOP.
4. **Set an internal timeline per step** that adds up to the turnaround time promised in Module 4 — if the steps don't actually fit inside that turnaround at the realistic capacity from Module 1, flag the mismatch and recommend adjusting either the turnaround promise or the process.
5. **Build in a scope-creep checkpoint** — a specific, explicit moment in the process (typically at intake) where an out-of-scope request gets caught and routed to Module 4's defined path (priced add-on, or declined/quoted separately) rather than silently absorbed into the fixed-price delivery.

## Output format

```
DELIVERY SOP (per tier, or shared where tiers overlap):

STEP 1 — Intake
Process: [what's collected, how — e.g. standard form]
Templated/automated: [what's reusable here]

STEP 2 — Production
Process: [ordered production steps]
Templated/automated: [what's reusable here]

STEP 3 — Quality Check
Process: [what's verified before delivery]

STEP 4 — Delivery/Handoff
Process: [how the deliverable reaches the client]

TIMELINE PER STEP: [adds up to Module 4's promised turnaround — flag if it doesn't]
SCOPE-CREEP CHECKPOINT: [where and how out-of-scope requests get caught and routed]
SINGLE-PERSON-DEPENDENCY FLAG: [any step that only works because of one person's judgment, not the documented process]
```

## Handoff

This SOP is what makes the package genuinely deliverable, not just sellable — Module 13 assembles it as an internal companion document alongside the client-facing package presentation from Module 12. Module 14 will specifically check whether this SOP is realistic against the price and capacity established in Modules 1 and 7.
