# Module 2: Offer Clarifier

**Purpose:** Sharpen the offer and the transformation it delivers before defining scope or price. A productized package built on a vague transformation just becomes a vague, harder-to-sell version of the same bespoke confusion it's meant to replace.

## Process

1. **State the current offer in one sentence**, as the user described it.
2. **Stress-test it against the Value Equation**: Dream Outcome (how big/specific is the result?), Likelihood of Success (how believable is it?), Time Delay (how fast do they get there?), Effort & Sacrifice (how easy is it, especially compared to a custom engagement?). Flag whichever of these four is weakest or missing.
3. **Rewrite the offer as a before → after transformation**, not a feature list. Bad: "we provide bookkeeping services." Good: "go from dreading tax season and guessing at your numbers to a clean, reconciled set of books delivered by the 5th of every month, with no surprises."
4. **Check specificity.** A productized service in particular benefits from a very concrete transformation, since the whole pitch is "you know exactly what you get" — vagueness here undercuts the core selling point of productizing at all.
5. **Flag anything unprovable or overclaimed** — this will surface again at Module 10 (Objection Handler) and matters especially here, since a productized offer implicitly promises consistency; an overclaim that can't be consistently delivered damages trust faster than a one-off bespoke project's occasional miss would.

## Output format

```
CURRENT OFFER (as given): [one sentence]
WEAKEST LINK: [Dream Outcome / Likelihood / Time / Effort]
SHARPENED OFFER (one sentence): [rewrite]
TRANSFORMATION STATEMENT (one paragraph, before → after): [rewrite]
RED FLAGS: [anything overclaimed, vague, or unprovable]
```

## Handoff

The sharpened offer and transformation statement become the backbone of Module 4 (Scope Definition), Module 6 (Tier Design), and Module 12 (Package Presentation). If the user pushes back on the sharpened version, ask which specific part felt wrong and refine, since a vague offer will make the scope boundary in Module 4 much harder to draw cleanly.
