# Module 10: Affiliate & Partner Enablement Kit

**Purpose:** Give anyone promoting the launch on the business's behalf everything they need to do it well — this only runs if Module 1/4 confirmed affiliates or partners are in scope, and its quality directly affects how much promotional reach the launch actually gets.

## Process

1. **Confirm scope from Module 4** — skip this module entirely if no affiliates/partners are involved in this launch.
2. **Write ready-to-use swipe copy**: 2-3 email swipe options (short and long versions) partners can send to their own list with minimal editing, a few social media post variants, and suggested subject lines — all grounded in the sharpened offer (Module 2) and VoC profile (Module 3) so partners aren't left guessing at positioning.
3. **Provide key facts partners need**: the exact cart-open/cart-close dates, the price and any fast-action bonus deadline (Module 9), the commission structure (if the user has one — ask if unclear), and links/tracking instructions.
4. **Provide brand guardrails** — claims that can and can't be made (from Module 1's constraints), so partner-generated promotion doesn't accidentally overclaim on the business's behalf.
5. **Package it as a single, scannable document** a partner can act on in a few minutes, not a dense brief they'd need to study.

## Output format

```
AFFILIATE/PARTNER KIT

KEY FACTS:
Cart open: [date] | Cart close: [date] | Price: [amount] | Fast-action bonus deadline: [date, if applicable]
Commission structure: [detail, or "confirm with the business owner"]
Tracking/links: [detail]

SWIPE COPY:
Email swipe (short): [ready-to-send copy]
Email swipe (long): [ready-to-send copy]
Social post variants: [2-3 short variants]

BRAND GUARDRAILS: [claims that can/can't be made]
```

## Handoff

This kit should be delivered to partners well before cart-open per Module 4's timeline, and included in Module 13's full assembly as a standalone package rather than folded into the buyer-facing materials.
