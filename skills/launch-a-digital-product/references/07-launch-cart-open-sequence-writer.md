# Module 7: Launch/Cart-Open Sequence Writer

**Purpose:** Write the email sequence that runs once the cart opens — driving the audience, now primed by the pre-launch content, to the sales page and to an actual purchase decision.

## Process

1. **Confirm sequence length and cadence from Module 4's map.** A typical cart-open sequence for a live launch runs 4-6 emails across the cart-open window: an opening "doors are open" announcement, 2-3 value/proof/objection-focused follow-ups, and a lead-in to the cart-close sequence (Module 8).
2. **Email 1 — Doors are open.** Announce plainly that the cart is open, link directly to the sales page (Module 6), and recap the transformation in a line or two — this reader has been primed by the pre-launch content, so this email's job is converting anticipation into action, not re-explaining from scratch.
3. **Follow-up emails — vary the angle, not just the reminder.** Each should add something new: a proof/case-study email, an objection-focused email (addressing the top hesitation from Module 3), a behind-the-scenes or founder-story email that deepens trust. Avoid "just checking in" filler — every email should give the reader a reason to open the next one.
4. **Reference the fast-action bonus** (once Module 9 runs) in at least one early cart-open email, since fast-action incentives lose their power if buried or announced too late in the window.
5. **End the sequence by leading directly into Module 8's cart-close messaging** — the last cart-open email should set up the shift in tone (from "here's why to buy" to "time is running out") rather than the cart-close sequence starting abruptly.

## Output format

```
CART-OPEN SEQUENCE ([count] emails):

EMAIL 1 — Doors Are Open
Body: [full email copy, linking to the sales page]

EMAIL 2 — [angle, e.g. Proof/Case Study]
Body: [full email copy]

EMAIL 3 — [angle, e.g. Objection-Focused]
Body: [full email copy]

(repeat per email)

LEAD-IN TO CART-CLOSE (final email in this sequence)
Body: [full email copy, setting up the tone shift]
```

## Handoff

This sequence feeds Module 13's assembly at the cart-open dates from Module 4's timeline. Module 8 (Cart-Close) should pick up directly from this sequence's final email rather than starting its own arc from zero.
