# Module 14: Launch Optimizer

**Purpose:** Score the finished launch package, improve the highest-leverage moments, and produce A/B variants — the final quality gate before the launch goes live against a real, public timeline.

## Process

1. **Score the assembled launch** on a simple rubric, 1-5 each:
   - **Anticipation strength** — does the pre-launch content genuinely build belief and excitement, or does it feel like filler before the pitch?
   - **Sales page quality** — carry forward the score from `sales-page-that-converts`'s own Page Optimizer stage (Module 6's handoff) rather than re-scoring it independently.
   - **Cart-open momentum** — does the cart-open sequence give real reasons to buy across its full run, or does it thin out after the first email?
   - **Urgency honesty** — is every deadline used (cart-close, fast-action bonus) genuinely real and enforced, with nothing fabricated?
   - **Delivery/onboarding strength** — does the post-purchase experience match the effort put into the pre-sale materials, or is it a visible drop-off?
   - **Affiliate readiness** (if in scope) — is the partner kit complete enough that a partner could act on it without follow-up questions?
2. **Flag the lowest-scoring 1-2 dimensions** and revisit those specific modules rather than doing a generic full-package polish — a low delivery/onboarding score, in particular, is worth fixing before launch even if the pre-sale materials all score well, since it directly drives refunds and the next launch's trust.
3. **Check the launch calendar for realistic timing** — are the gaps between pre-launch content, cart-open, and cart-close reasonable given list warmth and awareness stage (Module 3), or is the timeline rushed relative to what the audience needs to make a considered decision?
4. **Produce at least one A/B variant** — typically an alternate cart-open Email 1 (the highest-traffic moment of the whole launch) or an alternate fast-action bonus, since both have outsized leverage on total launch revenue. Note the hypothesis each variant tests.

## Output format

```
SCORES (1-5): Anticipation Strength:_ Sales Page Quality:_ Cart-Open Momentum:_ Urgency Honesty:_ Delivery/Onboarding Strength:_ Affiliate Readiness:_

TOP FIXES MADE: [list, tied to lowest scores]

TIMELINE REALISM CHECK: [any pacing concerns given list warmth/awareness stage]

A/B VARIANT:
- Cart-open Email 1 or fast-action bonus alternate: [detail]
- Hypothesis being tested: [one line]
```

## Handoff

This is the last stage — present the final scored launch package, the master calendar, and the A/B variant to the user as the finished deliverable, ready to schedule. If any score is 3 or below, say so plainly rather than presenting the launch as "done" — flag it as a known weak point and what it would take to fix (e.g. "Delivery/Onboarding Strength scored low because there's no clear first-win path defined yet — recommend fixing this before cart-open, since it directly affects refund rate").
