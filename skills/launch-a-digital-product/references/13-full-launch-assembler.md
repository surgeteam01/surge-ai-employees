# Module 13: Full Launch Assembler

**Purpose:** Assemble every prior module's output into one cohesive, ready-to-run launch package — the calendar, the content, the sales page, and every sequence — so the pipeline's output is something the user can actually execute against a real calendar.

## Process

1. **Follow the timeline from Module 4** as the master skeleton, dropping each piece into its dated slot: pre-launch content (Module 5), the sales page (Module 6), the cart-open sequence (Module 7), cart-close sequence (Module 8), fast-action bonus (Module 9), affiliate kit (Module 10), post-launch delivery (Module 11), and the evergreen transition plan (Module 12) if applicable.
2. **Smooth transitions between pieces** — the pre-launch content should lead naturally into cart-open, cart-open into cart-close, cart-close into post-purchase delivery — the whole launch should read as one coherent story with a clear beginning, middle, and end, not disconnected assets.
3. **Check voice consistency** against the brief from Module 1 throughout, particularly between the pre-launch content, the delegated sales page, and the launch emails, which were produced by different modules/pipelines.
4. **Verify nothing was lost**: every dated item on Module 4's timeline should have its content present, and any module marked "not applicable" (e.g. no affiliates, no evergreen transition) should be noted as intentionally skipped rather than silently missing.
5. **Produce a single master calendar view** at the top of the assembled output, so the user can see the whole launch at a glance before scrolling into the full content.

## Output format

```
=== LAUNCH CALENDAR ===
[Date] — [Piece] — [owning module]
(repeat per dated item from Module 4's timeline)

=== PRE-LAUNCH CONTENT ===
[from Module 5]

=== SALES PAGE ===
[from Module 6]

=== CART-OPEN SEQUENCE ===
[from Module 7]

=== CART-CLOSE SEQUENCE ===
[from Module 8, or "not applicable — evergreen launch"]

=== FAST-ACTION BONUS ===
[from Module 9, or "not included"]

=== AFFILIATE/PARTNER KIT ===
[from Module 10, or "not applicable — no affiliates in this launch"]

=== POST-LAUNCH DELIVERY & ONBOARDING ===
[from Module 11]

=== EVERGREEN TRANSITION PLAN ===
[from Module 12, or "not applicable — one-time live launch"]
```

## Handoff

This draft goes straight to Module 14 for scoring and optimization — don't present this as final without running it through the optimizer, since a first assembly pass almost always has at least one weak transition or an under-invested post-purchase experience relative to the pre-sale materials.
