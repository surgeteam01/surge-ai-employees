# Module 12: CTA & Close Writer

**Purpose:** Write the lead-capture CTAs used across the system — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`'s CTA & Close Writer logic.

## Process

1. **Write CTA copy for each capture point**: the lead magnet's landing page/form (Module 4), the chatbot's capture moment (Module 5), the outreach sequence's asks (Module 8), and the booking flow's final call-to-book (Module 10).
2. **Match CTA weight to context** — a lead magnet CTA can be direct ("get the checklist"); a chatbot CTA should feel like a natural next step in conversation; a booking CTA should be specific about what happens next ("book your 15-minute fit call").
3. **Vary CTA phrasing across touchpoints** so the system doesn't feel like the same button pasted everywhere.

## Output format

```
CTA BY TOUCHPOINT:
Lead magnet/landing page: [copy]
Chatbot capture moment: [copy]
Outreach sequence: [copy per touch]
Booking flow: [copy]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 13's assembly at each respective touchpoint.
