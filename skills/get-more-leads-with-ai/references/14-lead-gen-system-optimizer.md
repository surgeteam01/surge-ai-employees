# Module 14: Lead Gen System Optimizer

**Purpose:** Score the finished system on lead quality, automation realism, and tool fit, and produce test variants — the final quality gate before implementation.

## Process

1. **Score the assembled system** on a simple rubric, 1-5 each:
   - **Lead quality focus** — does the system genuinely filter for fit/intent (Module 6), or does it optimize purely for volume?
   - **Capture-to-value ratio** — does the lead magnet/chatbot deliver real value before asking for contact info, or does it feel extractive?
   - **Automation realism** — is what's automated actually achievable with Module 11's tool stack and Module 1's budget, or does it assume capabilities beyond what's realistic?
   - **Personalization honesty** — does the outreach (Module 8) claim more personalization than the actual data (Module 7) supports?
   - **Routing coherence** — do hot/warm/cold leads actually flow to the right track without gaps?
   - **Human handoff clarity** — is it clear at what point a real person takes over from automation?
2. **Flag the lowest-scoring 1-2 dimensions** and revisit those specific modules.
3. **Check specifically for over-automation risk** — a system that never hands off to a real human, or that over-claims personalization, risks feeling impersonal or dishonest to leads, which damages conversion and trust even if lead volume looks good on paper.
4. **Produce at least one test variant** — typically an alternate lead magnet or an alternate chatbot opening prompt, since both sit at the top of the funnel and have outsized leverage on total lead volume.

## Output format

```
SCORES (1-5): Lead Quality Focus:_ Capture-to-Value Ratio:_ Automation Realism:_ Personalization Honesty:_ Routing Coherence:_ Human Handoff Clarity:_

TOP FIXES MADE: [list, tied to lowest scores]

A/B VARIANT:
- Alternate lead magnet or chatbot opening: [detail]
- Hypothesis being tested: [one line]
```

## Handoff

Present the final scored system to the user as the finished deliverable, ready to implement against Module 11's tool stack. Flag any score of 3 or below plainly rather than presenting the system as "done."
