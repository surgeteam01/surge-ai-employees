# Module 2: Clarifying questions

**Purpose:** The fewest high-signal questions that take a vague goal to a confident route — never more than two before recommending. Speed is the whole value of a front door; an interview defeats it.

## When to ask nothing

If the request already matches one skill's trigger phrases in Module 1, or a journey in Module 3, recommend at once. Asking a question the founder has effectively already answered reads as not having listened.

If there is a Business Brain, hosted or file (Module 5 says where to look), read it before asking anything. It usually answers the second question below outright.

## The two questions that resolve almost everything

**1. "What do you want to have at the end of this?"**
For a broad outcome — "grow the business", "get more customers", "make more money". The answer names a deliverable, and a deliverable names a skill: a sales page, an email list, a month of content, a priced offer that does not exist yet.

**2. "Do you already know who this is for and what you sell them, or are you still working that out?"**
For deciding whether foundational work comes first. "Still working it out" is the answer: route to `find-your-niche` before anything else — every other skill's opening stage depends on it. A brain with *target audience* and *core offers* answered makes this question unnecessary.

## Secondary questions, only when the two above do not settle it

- **"Which channel are you serious about?"** — a content goal that could be YouTube, LinkedIn, a podcast, a faceless channel or general social.
- **"Is this new, or fixing something that exists?"** — a from-scratch pipeline (`launch-a-digital-product`) versus a narrower fix (just the pricing, just the ad copy).
- **"What is already built?"** — when "my funnel" or "my course" might be a thing that exists or an idea.

## What not to ask

- Anything a skill's own opening stage will collect — budget, platform specifics, voice. That work is done well there and badly here.
- A compound question. One clear question, one clear answer.
- A question whose answer would not change the route.

## Handoff

With the answers, check Module 3 for a journey, then hand off per Module 4.
