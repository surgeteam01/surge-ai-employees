# Module 5: Sales & Conversion Funnel Audit

**Purpose:** Trace the actual path from stranger to customer and find where it leaks — traffic and attention fixes are wasted if the funnel underneath doesn't convert what it already has.

## Process

1. **Map the current funnel stage by stage** — awareness, lead capture, nurture, sale — as it actually exists today, not as it's intended to work.
2. **Identify where drop-off is worst**, or flag that it's genuinely unknown (no tracking in place) — that absence is itself a finding.
3. **Assess sales page/pitch quality qualitatively** if one exists — clarity of offer, proof, objection handling, call to action.
4. **Check for a nurture mechanism at all** — a one-shot pitch with no follow-up for people who don't buy immediately is one of the most common and most fixable funnel gaps.

## Output format

```
FUNNEL MAP: [awareness → lead → nurture → sale, as it currently exists]
WEAKEST STAGE: [stage] — [why, or "unknown — no tracking in place"]
SALES ASSET QUALITY: [qualitative assessment, or "none exists"]
NURTURE MECHANISM: [exists and adequate / exists but weak / doesn't exist]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 12. A weak sales asset maps to `sales-page-that-converts`; a missing nurture mechanism maps to `build-your-email-funnel`; unknown drop-off maps to `business-analyst` for a deeper, data-driven funnel analysis once tracking exists.
