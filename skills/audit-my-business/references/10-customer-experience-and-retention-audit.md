# Module 10: Customer Experience & Retention Audit

**Purpose:** Check what happens after the sale — acquisition fixes elsewhere in this audit are wasted if customers churn quickly or never refer anyone.

## Process

1. **Assess onboarding/first-use experience** qualitatively — is there a defined first-use path, or does a new customer have to figure it out themselves?
2. **Check for a retention or repeat-purchase mechanism** — anything designed to bring a customer back, versus relying on one-time transactions.
3. **Check whether referral/word-of-mouth is being asked for** at all, or is happening (if at all) purely by accident.
4. **Flag if no feedback/satisfaction signal is being collected** — a business with no visibility into customer sentiment is flying blind on this whole area.

## Output format

```
ONBOARDING EXPERIENCE: [defined / ad hoc]
RETENTION MECHANISM: [exists — detail / doesn't exist]
REFERRAL SYSTEM: [actively asked for / happens by accident / doesn't happen]
FEEDBACK COLLECTION: [in place / not in place]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 12. Onboarding/retention gaps map to `course-creator`'s Student Success system pattern (adapted beyond courses) or `build-your-email-funnel`'s nurture logic, depending on business type.
