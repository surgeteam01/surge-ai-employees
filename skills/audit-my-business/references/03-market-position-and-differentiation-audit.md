# Module 3: Market Position & Differentiation Audit

**Purpose:** Check whether the business is actually differentiated and targeting a specific enough audience, since undifferentiated positioning quietly undermines every other area this skill audits — marketing, sales, and pricing all work harder than they should when positioning is vague.

## Process

1. **State the current audience/positioning** as described.
2. **Scan how differentiated it actually is** against direct competitors — adapted from `find-your-niche`'s Market Landscape Scan.
3. **Check whether messaging is specific or generic** — would the current pitch apply just as well to most competitors in the category?
4. **Flag "serves everyone" as a finding, not a strength** — a business without a defined audience isn't flexible, it's unfocused, and that shows up downstream in every marketing and sales metric.

## Output format

```
CURRENT AUDIENCE/POSITIONING: [as described]
DIFFERENTIATION SCAN: [what makes this genuinely different from 2-3 direct competitors, or "not clearly different"]
MESSAGING SPECIFICITY: [specific / generic — detail]
FLAG: [serves-everyone finding, or "positioning is reasonably specific"]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 12. A weak or generic positioning finding maps directly to `find-your-niche` as the recommended fix.
