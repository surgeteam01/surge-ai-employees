# Module 13: Full Audit Report Assembler

**Purpose:** Assemble every module's findings into one coherent report ready to present, not a stack of disconnected sections the reader has to synthesize themselves.

## Process

1. **Lead with an executive summary** — the top 3-5 findings, pulled from Module 12's "fix now" and highest-severity "plan for" items.
2. **Organize the body by business area**, matching Modules 2-11 in order.
3. **Attach the full opportunity matrix (Module 12)** as its own section.
4. **Verify every finding in the report has a mapped next step** — nothing should appear in the narrative sections without also appearing in the matrix.

## Output format

```
=== BUSINESS AUDIT REPORT ===

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
[Top 3-5 findings, plain language]

FINDINGS BY AREA
Offer & Business Model: [Module 2 summary]
Market Position: [Module 3 summary]
Marketing & Content: [Module 4 summary]
Sales & Funnel: [Module 5 summary]
Pricing & Profitability: [Module 6 summary]
Operations & Systems: [Module 7 summary]
Financial Health: [Module 8 summary + scope disclaimer]
Team & Capacity: [Module 9 summary]
Customer Experience & Retention: [Module 10 summary]
Risk & Vulnerability: [Module 11 summary]

=== OPPORTUNITY MATRIX ===
[from Module 12]
```

## Handoff

This draft goes straight to Module 14 for scoring before being presented as final.
