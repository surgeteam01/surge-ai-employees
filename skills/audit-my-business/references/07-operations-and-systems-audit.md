# Module 7: Operations & Systems Audit

**Purpose:** Assess whether delivery is repeatable and systemized or dependent on ad hoc founder effort — this caps how much the business can grow without breaking, regardless of how strong marketing or sales are.

## Process

1. **Check whether delivery follows a documented, repeatable process**, adapted from `productize-your-service`'s Delivery SOP logic, or is re-invented per client/order.
2. **Identify the single biggest bottleneck** — usually a founder-dependency where one person is a hard constraint on throughput.
3. **Inventory the current tool/software stack** for obvious gaps (a manual process that should be automated) or redundancy (paying for overlapping tools).

## Output format

```
DELIVERY PROCESS: [documented/repeatable / ad hoc per client]
BIGGEST BOTTLENECK: [detail — usually a specific person or step]
TOOL STACK: [gaps or redundancy flagged, or "no obvious issues"]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 12. A lack of a repeatable delivery process maps directly to `productize-your-service`.
