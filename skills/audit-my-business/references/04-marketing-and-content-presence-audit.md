# Module 4: Marketing & Content Presence Audit

**Purpose:** Assess whether marketing channels and content are actually reaching and converting the target audience, or just producing activity without a clear connection to the offer.

## Process

1. **Inventory active channels** — content, social platforms, SEO, paid ads, email.
2. **Check consistency/cadence** against what's realistic given the business's actual capacity (per Module 1's context), not an idealized posting schedule.
3. **Check whether content ties back to the offer** — activity that's disconnected from what the business actually sells is a common finding, not a rare one.
4. **Spot-check voice consistency** — cross-reference a `write-like-a-human` profile if one exists.
5. **Identify the single most underused high-potential channel** given the audience from Module 3, rather than recommending "do more everywhere."

## Output format

```
CHANNEL INVENTORY: [active channels + rough cadence on each]
CONSISTENCY ASSESSMENT: [sustainable / inconsistent — detail]
OFFER CONNECTION: [content clearly supports the offer / disconnected — detail]
VOICE CONSISTENCY: [consistent / inconsistent, or "no voice profile to check against"]
BIGGEST UNDERUSED OPPORTUNITY: [single channel + why]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 12. Findings map to the specific channel skill — `30-days-of-content`, `grow-on-youtube`, `build-your-personal-brand-on-linkedin`, `start-a-faceless-channel`, `launch-a-podcast`, or `rank-on-google` — matched to the underused opportunity identified.
