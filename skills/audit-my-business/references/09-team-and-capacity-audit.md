# Module 9: Team & Capacity Audit

**Purpose:** Check whether the people and capacity behind the business can actually support its current load or planned growth, since every other fix this audit recommends assumes someone has the capacity to execute it.

## Process

1. **Inventory the current team/contractor structure**, including a solo-founder-only setup.
2. **Identify the biggest capacity bottleneck** — a role nobody clearly owns, or a founder personally handling too much of delivery, marketing, and sales at once.
3. **Check hiring/delegation readiness** if growth is a stated goal from Module 1 — is the business structurally ready to hand work off, or would growth just increase the bottleneck's load?

## Output format

```
TEAM STRUCTURE: [detail]
BIGGEST CAPACITY BOTTLENECK: [detail]
GROWTH READINESS: [ready to delegate / not yet — why, only if growth is a stated goal]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 12. A severe capacity bottleneck should be weighted heavily in Module 12's matrix — it's often the constraint that makes every other recommended fix unactionable until it's addressed.
