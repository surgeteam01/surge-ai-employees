# Module 2: Offer & Business Model Audit

**Purpose:** Assess whether the core offer(s) and business model are actually sound before auditing anything downstream of them — a broken funnel or weak marketing matters less than a broken offer underneath it.

## Process

1. **Inventory the current offer(s)** as the user describes them.
2. **Check offer clarity** — can the core offer be stated in one sentence? If not, that's itself a finding, not just a communication issue.
3. **Check for offer sprawl** — too many offers diluting focus and confusing the buyer's decision, versus a focused core offer with clear extensions.
4. **Assess revenue model soundness** — one-time versus recurring, and whether the margin structure implied by Module 1's revenue stage actually supports the business.
5. **Flag concentration risk** — heavy reliance on a single client, product, or offer for most of revenue.

## Output format

```
OFFER INVENTORY: [list of current offers]
OFFER CLARITY: [clear one-sentence statement, or "unclear" + why]
OFFER SPRAWL: [focused / diluted — detail]
REVENUE MODEL ASSESSMENT: [one-time/recurring, margin soundness]
CONCENTRATION RISK: [flagged dependency, or "none apparent"]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 6 (pricing builds on this offer assessment) and Module 12 (the opportunity matrix). Offer-clarity and sprawl flags map to `find-your-niche` or `productize-your-service`; revenue-model flags map to `nail-your-pricing`.
