# Module 9: Objection Handler

**Purpose:** Surface the real objections a buyer will have about this specific price and answer them directly. Price objections are the most common objection of all — a rebuttal that's actually ready in advance is the difference between losing the sale and closing it.

## Process

1. **Start from Module 3's anticipated price objections**, then add the categories that apply to any priced offer: "it's too expensive" (compared to what, specifically — surface the implicit comparison), "can I get a discount," "why is this more than [competitor]," "I need to think about it / check with someone," and "what if it doesn't work for me at this price."
2. **Write a direct rebuttal for each** — acknowledge the concern as real before answering it; a defensive or dismissive answer to a price objection reads as confirming the price is indeed too high.
3. **Tie every price rebuttal back to the transformation and value stack** (Modules 2 and 6), not to a defensive justification of the number itself — "here's what this actually solves for you" lands better than "well, it took a lot of work to build."
4. **Handle the discount request explicitly.** Decide and state a clear policy: no discounting (protects price integrity and margin), a structured discount only under specific conditions (annual prepay, referral, cohort timing), or case-by-case at the business's discretion — an ad hoc "maybe, let me think about it" response undermines pricing confidence more than a firm "no" does.

## Output format

```
OBJECTIONS HANDLED:
1. [Objection] → [Rebuttal, tied back to transformation/value] → context: [when this typically comes up]
2. ...

DISCOUNT POLICY: [no discounting / structured discount conditions / case-by-case] — reasoning: [why this protects margin and price integrity]

FAQ (customer-phrased):
Q: [price-related question in customer's actual words]
A: [direct, honest answer]
(repeat)
```

## Handoff

These rebuttals feed Module 12's presentation copy (the strongest 1-2 objections should be pre-empted directly on the pricing page/sheet) and should be handed to the user as a ready reference for live sales conversations, alongside the guarantee from Module 10, which resolves several of these objections on its own.
