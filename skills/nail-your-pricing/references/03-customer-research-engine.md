# Module 3: Customer Research Engine

**Purpose:** Build a Voice-of-Customer (VoC) profile with a specific focus on price sensitivity and willingness-to-pay signals — so the eventual number is grounded in what this audience actually values and can afford, not just what feels right.

## Process

1. **Pull from real material first**, if any exists: past sales conversations, pricing objections actually received, competitor comparisons customers have mentioned, or survey data. Ask the user if any of this exists before generating from scratch.
2. **If no real material exists**, build a hypothesis-based profile from the audience description and general market knowledge — label it clearly as a hypothesis and recommend validating the eventual price against 5-10 real conversations or a small test cohort before a full launch.
3. **Identify willingness-to-pay signals**: what this audience currently spends on adjacent solutions (including doing nothing / the cost of the unsolved problem), whether they're price-sensitive individual buyers or less price-sensitive business/expense-account buyers, and whether the purchase is typically a considered decision or an impulse one.
4. **Identify the buyer awareness stage** (Eugene Schwartz's model) since it affects how much the price needs to be pre-justified before it's shown:
   - *Unaware / Problem-aware* — needs more value-building before a price will land; a bare price with no context reads as arbitrary.
   - *Solution-aware / Product-aware* — some value context still needed, but comparison to alternatives can do more of the work.
   - *Most-aware* — already sold on the category and considering this option; price can be presented more directly.
5. **Extract exact phrases** the audience uses when discussing cost, value, or budget — these inform Module 9's objection rebuttals and Module 12's justification language.

## Output format

```
AWARENESS STAGE: [one of the five above, with a one-line justification]

WILLINGNESS-TO-PAY SIGNALS:
- Current spend on adjacent solutions / cost of the problem unsolved: [detail]
- Buyer type: [individual/price-sensitive vs business/expense-account]
- Purchase pattern: [considered decision vs impulse]

TOP PRICE-RELATED OBJECTIONS (anticipated):
- ...

EXACT-PHRASE BANK (how the audience talks about cost/value):
- ...

CONFIDENCE: [Real data / Hypothesis — recommend validation]
```

## Handoff

Willingness-to-pay signals and awareness stage feed directly into Module 4 (choosing a pricing model/strategy) and Module 8 (setting the actual number within a defensible range). The objections and phrase bank feed Module 9 and Module 12.
