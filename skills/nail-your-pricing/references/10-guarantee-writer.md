# Module 10: Guarantee Writer

**Purpose:** Write an ethical, confidence-building guarantee that reduces the buyer's perceived risk at this specific price point without over-promising or creating a claim the business can't honor.

## Process

1. **Confirm what the business can actually stand behind** — never write a guarantee stronger than what the user has confirmed they'll honor, and check it against any constraint already flagged in Module 1 (an existing guarantee commitment this must not contradict). If unclear, ask before drafting.
2. **Choose a guarantee type that fits the offer and the price point**, since a higher price generally needs a stronger risk-reversal to feel proportionate:
   - **Unconditional** (e.g. "30-day money back, no questions asked") — highest trust, best when confident in product-market fit.
   - **Conditional** (refund if the buyer does X and doesn't get Y) — reduces risk of refund abuse, still builds confidence.
   - **Performance-based** (tied to a specific measurable outcome) — strongest trust signal, only use if the outcome is genuinely reliable and measurable; especially powerful for premium positioning (Module 4), since it directly answers "is this worth the higher price?"
   - **Extended/double guarantee** (e.g. "keep the bonuses even if you refund") — useful when the core guarantee alone feels standard relative to the price.
3. **Write the guarantee in plain, warm language** — avoid legalistic hedging that undercuts the confidence it's meant to convey, while staying accurate to the real terms.
4. **Weigh the guarantee's cost against margin** (from Module 1) — a generous guarantee at a thin margin needs a realistic refund-rate assumption behind it; flag if the guarantee type chosen carries real financial risk worth the user knowingly accepting.

## Output format

```
GUARANTEE TYPE: [chosen type] — reason: [fit with offer, price point, and business stage]
GUARANTEE COPY: [2-4 sentences, ready to place alongside the price]
FINE PRINT (if needed): [any necessary conditions, written plainly]
MARGIN RISK NOTE: [any real financial exposure worth flagging given Module 1's margin data]
```

## Handoff

This feeds Module 11 (payment structure may reference the guarantee period) and Module 12 (it typically sits directly beside or under the price itself) and doubles as a rebuttal to the price/trust objections surfaced in Module 9.
