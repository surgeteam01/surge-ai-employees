# Module 2: Offer Clarifier

**Purpose:** Sharpen the offer and the transformation it delivers before any pricing decision gets made. A vague offer produces an arbitrary price — you can't set a defensible number for something that isn't clearly defined yet.

## Process

1. **State the current offer in one sentence**, as the user described it.
2. **Stress-test it against the Value Equation**: Dream Outcome (how big/specific is the result?), Likelihood of Success (how believable is it?), Time Delay (how fast do they get there?), Effort & Sacrifice (how easy is it?). Flag whichever of these four is weakest or missing — this matters more here than anywhere else in the pipeline, since the strength of the transformation is the single biggest lever on what price the market will actually bear.
3. **Rewrite the offer as a before → after transformation**, not a feature list. Bad: "website design services." Good: "go from a site that doesn't convert visitors into leads to one that turns 1 in 20 visitors into a booked call, launched within 3 weeks."
4. **Check specificity.** A vague promise can't be priced with confidence, because neither the seller nor the buyer has a clear sense of what's actually being paid for. Push for numbers, timeframes, and named outcomes wherever honestly possible.
5. **Flag anything unprovable or overclaimed** — an inflated promise used to justify a high price is a liability twice over: it surfaces as a credibility problem in Module 9 (Objection Handler), and if believed and then unmet, it drives refunds and damages the guarantee's credibility in Module 10.

## Output format

```
CURRENT OFFER (as given): [one sentence]
WEAKEST LINK: [Dream Outcome / Likelihood / Time / Effort]
SHARPENED OFFER (one sentence): [rewrite]
TRANSFORMATION STATEMENT (one paragraph, before → after): [rewrite]
RED FLAGS: [anything overclaimed, vague, or unprovable]
```

## Handoff

The sharpened offer and transformation statement become the backbone of Module 4 (Pricing Model Selector), Module 6 (Offer Stack Builder), and Module 12 (Presentation Writer) — the price is justified against this transformation, not against the deliverables alone. If the user pushes back on the sharpened version, ask which specific part felt wrong and refine, since a vague offer will make every downstream pricing decision feel arbitrary rather than earned.
