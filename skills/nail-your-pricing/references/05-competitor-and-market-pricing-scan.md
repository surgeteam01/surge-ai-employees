# Module 5: Competitor & Market Pricing Scan

**Purpose:** Establish real anchor points from the competitive landscape so the eventual price is set relative to the actual market, not in a vacuum — buyers always evaluate a price against alternatives, whether the seller accounts for that or not.

## Process

1. **Gather known competitor pricing** from Module 1's brief. If marked "unknown," work with the user to identify 3-5 realistic comparison points — direct competitors offering something similar, and adjacent alternatives (including the cost of the status quo/doing nothing, and DIY alternatives) that a buyer would mentally compare against even if they're not a perfect match.
2. **Categorize each comparison point**: cheaper-and-lesser (fewer features/less service, justifiably cheaper), similar-and-comparable (a real head-to-head), or premium-and-more (a higher-tier alternative that can make this offer look reasonably priced by comparison).
3. **Identify positioning gaps** — is there a segment of the market being underserved at a certain price point? Is everyone clustered at one number, leaving room to differentiate above or below? Is the market anchored high (making a mid-price look like a bargain) or anchored low (making a premium claim harder to defend)?
4. **Flag when true price data isn't available** (many competitors don't publish pricing) — recommend realistic proxies: published pricing where available, typical market rates from industry sources, or the user's own experience quoting against these competitors in real sales conversations.

## Output format

```
COMPETITOR/MARKET LANDSCAPE:
[Competitor/alternative] — Price: [$x or "not published — estimate: $x"] — Category: [cheaper-and-lesser / comparable / premium]
(repeat per comparison point)

POSITIONING GAP: [where there's room to differentiate, and why]
MARKET ANCHOR: [high / low / clustered — implication for how this offer's price will be perceived]
DATA CONFIDENCE: [real published pricing / estimated / needs research]
```

## Handoff

This scan sets the realistic price range Module 8 works within, and directly informs Module 4's positioning choice if it wasn't already locked in. Module 12 (Presentation Writer) may also reference relevant comparisons directly in the pricing copy if doing so strengthens the value case without disparaging competitors unfairly.
