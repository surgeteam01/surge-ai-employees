## Reading the table

- A skill that reads a field will not ask for it when the file answers it.
- A skill that hands a field back is the one to run when that field is `_unanswered_` or stale: `write-like-a-human` for the whole voice part, `find-your-niche` for who the business serves, `nail-your-pricing` or `productize-your-service` for the offers, `build-your-own-brand-identity` for the visual part, `audit-my-business` for current goals and challenges.
- "—" means the skill produces a deliverable without settling a standing fact; it still hands back anything new the founder said.

## Handoff

None. Module 4 says how a hand-back reaches the brain: an import, as a draft, where a Business Brain tool is available; a fold into the file everywhere else.
