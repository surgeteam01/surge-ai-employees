# Module 9: Retention & Engagement Mechanics Designer

**Purpose:** Design pattern interrupts and open loops adapted for audio-only listening — adapted from `grow-on-youtube`/`start-a-faceless-channel`'s equivalent module, relying on structural and conversational variety in place of visual cuts.

## Process

1. **Design pattern interrupts using structural variety** from Module 7 — a topic/segment change, a story break, a shift in energy or pacing, since there's no visual cut available to renew attention.
2. **Design at least one open loop** — a teaser planted in the cold open or early in the episode ("later, [guest] tells me the one thing that almost made them quit") resolved later, which is an especially strong mechanic for audio since listeners often can't easily skim ahead the way a video viewer might scrub a timeline.
3. **Design engagement prompts** placed at natural pause points — a specific ask (leave a review mentioning a specific detail, share with a specific type of person who'd benefit) rather than a generic "please subscribe."
4. **Note this matters more for longer episodes** — a 60+ minute conversation needs more deliberate structural variety than a tight 20-minute solo episode to sustain full-episode listening.

## Output format

```
PATTERN INTERRUPTS: [tied to Module 7's structural variety points]
OPEN LOOP: [planted in cold open/early] → [resolved later]
ENGAGEMENT PROMPT: [specific ask] — placement: [natural pause point]
```
