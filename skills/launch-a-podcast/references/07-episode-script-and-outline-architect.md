# Module 7: Episode Script/Outline Architect

**Purpose:** Structure the full episode for audio-only retention — adapted from `start-a-faceless-channel`'s Narration Script Architect, adjusted for the conversational (interview) or monologue (solo) format podcasts actually use, rather than tightly scripted narration.

## Process

1. **Confirm the premise the packaging (Module 6) promised.**
2. **Structure with a clear arc**: a cold open (a short, compelling clip or statement — often pulled from later in the episode — played before the formal intro, since podcast apps show a preview and the first moments determine whether a listener commits), the formal intro (brief, consistent across episodes per Module 10's branding), the main content (segments for solo shows, a loose question flow for interviews — not a rigid script, since natural conversation retains listeners better than a read script for interview formats), and an outro.
3. **Write for spoken, not written, delivery** — shorter sentences, natural speech patterns; for interview shows, this module produces a flexible outline/question flow rather than a word-for-word script, since real conversation needs room to follow unexpected, valuable tangents.
4. **Plan pacing for a single continuous audio track** — since there's no visual cutting to renew attention the way video has, rely more heavily on structural variety (a change of topic/segment, a story break, a brief personal tangent) at reasonable intervals to prevent monotony over a longer listening session.
5. **Plan the ending deliberately** — pointing to the next episode or a specific CTA (feeds Module 12), not trailing off.

## Output format

```
EPISODE OUTLINE (target length: [x]):

COLD OPEN: [short compelling clip/statement, often pulled from later content]
INTRO: [brief, consistent format]

MAIN CONTENT:
[Solo format] Segment 1 [~x min]: [content] — pacing note: [structural variety point]
(repeat per segment)
[Interview format] Question flow: [ordered list of core questions/topics, with room noted for natural tangents]

OUTRO: [consistent format, points to next episode/CTA]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 9 (retention mechanics layered onto this structure) and, for interview episodes, is the direct input to Module 8's interview prep brief.
