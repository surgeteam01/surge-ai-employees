# Module 6: Episode Title & Artwork Packaging Lab

**Purpose:** Design the title, episode artwork (if per-episode art is used), and description for each episode — adapted from `grow-on-youtube`'s Title & Thumbnail Packaging Lab and `start-a-faceless-channel`'s no-face thumbnail approach, since podcast apps reward the same click-through mechanic through a different visual surface (a title and small artwork thumbnail in a scrollable list, not a large video thumbnail).

## Process

1. **Generate 4-6 title options per episode**, drawing on the idea/angle (Module 5) and Module 3's search/browse phrasing — for interview episodes, include the guest's name/credibility marker where it adds pull ("How [Guest], who bootstrapped to $1M with no ads, actually did it" beats a guest-name-only title with no hook).
2. **Write the episode description** with a strong opening 1-2 sentences (many podcast apps truncate descriptions similarly to social platforms) followed by a fuller summary, guest bio/links if applicable, and timestamps for longer episodes.
3. **Recommend episode artwork** if the show uses per-episode art (many don't, using a consistent show-level cover instead — confirm which approach fits Module 10's branding plan) — simple, high-contrast, legible at a very small size, since podcast artwork is viewed far smaller than a YouTube thumbnail.
4. **Score each title** on Curiosity, Clarity, and Honesty, consistent with the video-based skills' rubric, adjusted for a text-first, small-artwork context rather than a large visual thumbnail.

## Output format

```
EPISODE: [from Module 5]
TITLE OPTIONS (scored 1-5 on Curiosity/Clarity/Honesty): [...]
TOP TITLE: [...]
DESCRIPTION: [opening 1-2 sentences, full summary, guest bio/links if applicable, timestamps]
ARTWORK RECOMMENDATION (if per-episode art used): [...]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 7 (episode must deliver on the title's promise) and Module 11 (title language doubles as search/browse targeting).
