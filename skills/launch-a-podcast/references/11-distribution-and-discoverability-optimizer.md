# Module 11: Distribution & Discoverability Optimizer

**Purpose:** Design how the show actually gets discovered — a genuinely new mechanic in this repo, since podcast discovery works meaningfully differently from a video platform's recommendation algorithm: it leans heavily on directory categorization, reviews/ratings, and cross-promotion between shows rather than an algorithmic feed.

## Process

1. **Confirm directory submission and categorization** — the show should be submitted to every major platform relevant to Module 1's brief (Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and others), placed in the most accurate and competitive-but-relevant category and subcategory, since category browsing and charts are a real discovery surface podcast apps offer that most video platforms don't.
2. **Design a reviews/ratings strategy** — reviews and ratings are one of the strongest trust and discovery signals in podcast apps; recommend a specific, natural ask (in the outro per Module 10, and in early follow-up with engaged listeners) rather than hoping it happens organically.
3. **Design a cross-promotion strategy** — guest-swaps with adjacent shows (appearing as a guest on other podcasts serving a similar or adjacent audience, and hosting reciprocal guests), audio ad-swaps between shows, and shout-outs — cross-promotion between shows is one of the most reliable levers for podcast audience growth precisely because algorithmic discovery is weaker here than on video/social platforms.
4. **Note if a video/YouTube version is in scope** (from Module 1) — many shows now publish a video version of audio episodes to also compete for YouTube's separate discovery/algorithm surface; if so, note that `grow-on-youtube`'s packaging and SEO modules should be run for that version rather than assuming the audio-platform strategy alone covers it.

## Output format

```
DIRECTORY SUBMISSION: [platforms confirmed] — category/subcategory: [x]
REVIEWS/RATINGS STRATEGY: [specific ask, placement in outro + follow-up]
CROSS-PROMOTION STRATEGY: [target adjacent shows for guest-swaps, ad-swaps, shout-outs]
VIDEO VERSION (if in scope): [note to run grow-on-youtube's packaging/SEO modules separately]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 13's assembly as a standing growth strategy, separate from individual episode content.
