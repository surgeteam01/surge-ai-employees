# Module 8: Guest Booking & Interview Prep Guide

**Purpose:** Design the outreach approach for booking guests and a real preparation brief for the interview itself — this only applies to interview-format shows, and it's the module with no equivalent anywhere else in this repo, since no other skill deals with securing and prepping a specific outside participant for recorded content.

## Process

1. **Confirm this module applies** — skip entirely for solo/monologue shows.
2. **Identify the ideal guest profile** for each episode's angle (from Module 5) — specific enough to search for real candidates (a founder who's done X, an expert with Y specific experience), not a vague category.
3. **Design the outreach approach.** For a small number of specific guest targets, a warm, personalized pitch works well — reference `land-clients-with-cold-outreach`'s Opening Line & Hook Lab and Message Sequence Writer directly rather than re-deriving cold outreach mechanics here, adapted to pitch an interview slot rather than a sale (the "offer" being pitched is the exposure/platform value to the guest, which should be honestly and specifically articulated, not oversold).
4. **Write a real interview prep brief per booked guest**: research on their background and relevant work, a tailored question bank (grounded in Module 7's question-flow outline but personalized to this specific guest's actual experience, not generic), and a short pre-interview brief to send the guest covering format, length, and what to expect — reduces guest nervousness and produces a better episode.
5. **Note a realistic booking lead time** and build it into Module 4's calendar — rushed guest booking produces weaker episodes and last-minute cancellations.

## Output format

```
GUEST BOOKING (per interview episode):
Ideal guest profile: [specific, searchable]
Outreach approach: [warm/personalized pitch — reference land-clients-with-cold-outreach's hook/sequence logic, adapted to an interview-slot pitch]
Booking lead time: [x weeks]

INTERVIEW PREP BRIEF (once booked):
Guest research: [specific background/work relevant to the episode's angle]
Tailored question bank: [personalized, grounded in Module 7's outline]
Pre-interview brief to send guest: [format, length, what to expect]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 7 (the question flow gets personalized here) and Module 13's assembly as a per-episode operational document, separate from the publishable episode content itself.
