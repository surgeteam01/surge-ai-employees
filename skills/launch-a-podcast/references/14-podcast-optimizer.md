# Module 14: Podcast Optimizer

**Purpose:** Score the finished production plan on hook strength, retention design, guest-prep completeness, and discoverability — the final quality gate before recording/publishing begins.

## Process

1. **Score the assembled plan** on a simple rubric, 1-5 each:
   - **Hook/cold-open strength** — does each episode's cold open create genuine reason to keep listening?
   - **Packaging-to-content consistency** — does the outline genuinely deliver on the title/description's promise?
   - **Retention structure** — is there enough structural variety to sustain full-episode listening, especially for longer episodes?
   - **Guest-prep completeness** (if interview-based) — is the prep brief specific and personalized, or generic enough that any guest could receive the same one?
   - **Brand consistency** — does the intro/outro/segment structure hold consistent across the sampled episodes?
   - **Discoverability** — is the distribution/cross-promotion strategy concrete and actionable, or vague?
2. **Flag the lowest-scoring 1-2 dimensions** and revise those specific modules.
3. **Check guest-prep quality specifically for interview episodes** — a generic prep brief is one of the most common ways an interview episode underperforms despite a strong guest and topic.
4. **Produce at least one A/B variant** — typically an alternate title for the plan's highest-potential episode, or an alternate cross-promotion target.

## Output format

```
SCORES (1-5): Hook/Cold-Open Strength:_ Packaging-to-Content Consistency:_ Retention Structure:_ Guest-Prep Completeness:_ Brand Consistency:_ Discoverability:_

TOP FIXES MADE: [list, tied to lowest scores]

A/B VARIANT:
- Alternate title or cross-promotion target: [detail]
- Hypothesis being tested: [one line]
```

## Handoff

Present the final scored plan to the user as the finished deliverable, ready for recording/production. Flag any score of 3 or below plainly rather than presenting the plan as "done."
