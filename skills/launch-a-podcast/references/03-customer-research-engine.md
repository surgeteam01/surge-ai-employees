# Module 3: Customer Research Engine

**Purpose:** Build a Voice-of-Customer profile — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`, adapted toward what listeners search for in podcast apps specifically (which weight differently than YouTube or web search).

## Process

1. **Pull from real material first**: reviews of the show or similar shows, listener questions, community discussion. Ask before generating from scratch.
2. **If none exists**, build a hypothesis-based profile, labeled as such.
3. **Identify what this audience searches for in podcast apps** — often broader category/topic terms than granular long-tail search, since podcast app search and browse behavior differs from YouTube/Google's more specific query patterns.
4. **Extract exact phrases and recurring questions.**

## Output format

```
TOP PAINS (ranked): [...]
TOP DESIRES (ranked): [...]
PODCAST APP SEARCH/BROWSE BEHAVIOR: [what this audience looks for in show/episode titles and categories]
EXACT-PHRASE BANK: [...]
CONFIDENCE: [Real data / Hypothesis]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 5 (ideas), Module 6 (titles), and Module 11 (distribution/categories).
