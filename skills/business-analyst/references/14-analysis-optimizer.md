# Module 14: Analysis Optimizer

**Purpose:** Score the finished report on rigor, clarity, and actionability, and identify what would strengthen confidence further — the final quality gate before presenting to stakeholders.

## Process

1. **Score the assembled report** on a simple rubric, 1-5 each:
   - **Question-answer alignment** — does the report actually and directly answer Module 3's sharpened question?
   - **Rigor** — are alternative explanations genuinely ruled out (Module 11), or just asserted away?
   - **Honesty about uncertainty** — are confidence levels and data quality limitations stated plainly, or smoothed over into false certainty?
   - **Clarity for the audience** — is the executive summary genuinely readable by Module 1's intended audience without needing the full methodology?
   - **Actionability** — are Module 12's recommendations concrete enough to actually assign and measure, or still vague?
   - **Visual clarity** — do the charts (Module 8) support the findings clearly, or add clutter without adding clarity?
2. **Flag the lowest-scoring 1-2 dimensions** and revisit those specific modules.
3. **Identify what additional data or analysis would meaningfully strengthen confidence** in the weakest findings — a concrete, specific next step (not a vague "more research needed"), so the reader knows exactly what would move a "medium confidence" finding to "high confidence."
4. **Never inflate confidence to make the report seem more conclusive than the data supports** — a report that's honest about its limits is more useful and more trustworthy than one presented with artificial certainty.

## Output format

```
SCORES (1-5): Question-Answer Alignment:_ Rigor:_ Honesty About Uncertainty:_ Clarity for Audience:_ Actionability:_ Visual Clarity:_

TOP FIXES MADE: [list, tied to lowest scores]

WHAT WOULD STRENGTHEN CONFIDENCE FURTHER: [specific additional data/analysis, tied to the weakest findings]
```

## Handoff

This is the last stage — present the final scored report to the user as the finished deliverable. If any score is 3 or below, flag it plainly rather than presenting the analysis as fully conclusive when it isn't.
