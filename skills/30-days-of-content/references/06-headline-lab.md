# Module 6: Headline Lab

**Purpose:** Write and score post titles/hooks for each day's content — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`, since the underlying formulas (4 U's, benefit+timeframe, direct promise, curiosity, customer-language mirror) work identically whether the headline sits atop a sales page or a single social post.

## Process

1. **Generate 3-5 hook/title options per post** (fewer than a sales page headline, since this repeats 30 times across the calendar), drawing on the pillar's job (Module 4), the top pain/desire and exact-phrase bank (Module 3), and multiple formulas so options are genuinely different.
2. **Score each option** on: Clarity, Specificity, Believability, and Relevance to the specific pillar and post type — a promotional post's hook can be more direct, an educational post's hook should promise a specific, useful takeaway.
3. **Vary hook style across the 30 days deliberately** — don't let every post use the same formula (e.g. all questions, or all "X mistakes to avoid" lists); variety in hook style is itself part of what keeps a feed from feeling repetitive.
4. **Pick the top option per post** and note why it wins.

## Output format

```
POST: [Day n, pillar]
HOOK/TITLE OPTIONS (scored 1-5 on Clarity/Specificity/Believability/Relevance):
1. [hook] — C:_ S:_ B:_ R:_
2. ...
TOP PICK: [hook]
```

## Handoff

The top pick feeds Module 7's opening line (for formats where a hook and opening line are distinct, like a caption or article) and Module 9's platform adaptation.
