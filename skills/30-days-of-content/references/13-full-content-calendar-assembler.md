# Module 13: Full Content Calendar Assembler

**Purpose:** Assemble every prior module's output into one cohesive, ready-to-produce 30-day content calendar — every day's content, platform-adapted, with hooks, CTAs, and the repurposing/batching system as an operational companion.

## Process

1. **Follow Module 5's skeleton** as the master structure, dropping each day's content (hook from Module 6, lead from Module 7, idea from Module 8, platform adaptation from Module 9, proof content from Module 10 where applicable, CTA from Module 11) into place.
2. **Include the repurposing/batching system (Module 12)** as a clearly separated operational section, distinct from the day-by-day content itself.
3. **Check pillar variety visually** — scan across all 30 days to confirm pillars and post types are genuinely spread out, not clustered, matching Module 4's intended ratio.
4. **Check voice consistency** against the brief from Module 1 (or the linked `write-like-a-human` profile) throughout.
5. **Verify nothing was lost**: every day should have content, a hook, and a CTA; every platform in scope should have its adaptations present.

## Output format

```
=== 30-DAY CONTENT CALENDAR ===

DAY 1 — Pillar: [x] — Platform(s): [x]
Hook: [x]
Content: [platform-adapted, full content]
CTA: [x]

(repeat for all 30 days)

=== REPURPOSING & BATCHING SYSTEM ===
[from Module 12]
```

## Handoff

This draft goes straight to Module 14 for scoring and optimization — don't present this as final without running it through the optimizer, since a first assembly pass can still carry pillar clustering, repetitive hook styles, or a production load that isn't actually sustainable.
