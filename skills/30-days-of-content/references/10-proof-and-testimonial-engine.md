# Module 10: Proof & Testimonial Engine

**Purpose:** Turn raw results and testimonials into content for the "authority/proof" pillar days specifically — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`, since the underlying work (extracting a specific outcome, writing it up persuasively) is identical.

## Process

1. **Categorize available proof**: results/case studies, social proof (volume), authority proof (credentials, media), peer proof (testimonials).
2. **Match proof to the specific "authority/proof" pillar days** identified in Module 5's skeleton — each of these days needs a real piece of proof behind it, not a generic claim.
3. **Extract the specific outcome** from each raw testimonial without inventing anything. If a testimonial is vague, ask for the specific result or use it only as supporting color.
4. **Write each proof piece as its own content-ready post** (not just a quote to insert), following the format needs from Module 9 (a case-study carousel, a results-focused caption, etc.).

## Output format

```
PROOF-BASED CONTENT (for authority/proof pillar days):

DAY [n]
Proof type: [Result/Social/Authority/Peer]
Content: [full, content-ready write-up of this proof piece]

(repeat per authority/proof day in the calendar)

GAPS: [any authority/proof day that doesn't have real proof available — flag and recommend a substitute pillar assignment for that day instead of inventing placeholder proof]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 13's assembly directly at the days Module 5 assigned to the authority/proof pillar.
