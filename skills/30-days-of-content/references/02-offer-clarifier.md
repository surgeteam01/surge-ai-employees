# Module 2: Offer Clarifier

**Purpose:** Sharpen the underlying offer and transformation before generating content — even content that isn't directly selling should build toward something, and a vague underlying offer produces content that entertains or informs without ever compounding into business results.

## Process

1. **State the current offer in one sentence**, as the user described it.
2. **Stress-test it against the Value Equation**: Dream Outcome, Likelihood of Success, Time Delay, Effort & Sacrifice. Flag whichever is weakest.
3. **Rewrite the offer as a before → after transformation.** This transformation is what content pillars (Module 4) should orbit around, even indirectly — a content calendar's job is to build the audience's belief in this transformation over time, not to pitch it in every post.
4. **Check specificity.** A vague transformation produces vague content pillars; push for something concrete.
5. **Flag anything unprovable or overclaimed** — content is where a business's claims get the most repeated public exposure, so an overclaim here compounds risk faster than it would in a single sales page.

## Output format

```
CURRENT OFFER (as given): [one sentence]
WEAKEST LINK: [Dream Outcome / Likelihood / Time / Effort]
SHARPENED OFFER (one sentence): [rewrite]
TRANSFORMATION STATEMENT (one paragraph, before → after): [rewrite]
RED FLAGS: [anything overclaimed, vague, or unprovable]
```

## Handoff

The sharpened offer and transformation feed Module 4 (Content Pillar Architect) directly — every pillar should trace back to some facet of this transformation, even if only a fraction of posts pitch it directly.
