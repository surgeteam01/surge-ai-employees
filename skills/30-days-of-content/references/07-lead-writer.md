# Module 7: Lead Writer

**Purpose:** Write the opening line(s) that earn the rest of each post being read — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`, since the underlying lead styles (PAS, story/scene opener, bold claim + proof tease, direct address) apply to a social caption or article opening exactly as they do to a sales page lead.

## Process

1. **Pick a lead style matched to the post's pillar and type** (from Module 4): educational posts often work well with a direct-address or bold-claim opener; engagement/relatable posts often work well with a story/scene opener; authority/proof posts work well with a bold claim + proof tease.
2. **Use the exact-phrase bank from Module 3** in the opening lines — mirroring the audience's own words is one of the strongest scroll-stopping techniques available.
3. **Keep it short** — a social post's opening line has even less patience-runway than a sales page's lead; the first sentence needs to earn the second within a beat, not build slowly.
4. **Vary lead style across the 30 days**, same principle as Module 6 — repeating the same opening pattern across many posts flattens the feed's variety.

## Output format

```
POST: [Day n, pillar]
LEAD (style: [PAS / story / bold-claim / direct-address]): [1-3 sentences]
```

## Handoff

This opening line, paired with Module 6's hook, becomes the seed for Module 8's full idea generation and Module 9's format adaptation.
