# Module 8: Content Idea Generator

**Purpose:** Generate the actual 30 distinct content ideas, one per day in Module 5's skeleton, using the pillar assignments, hooks, and leads already produced — this is where the calendar goes from a strategic skeleton to specific, writeable content.

## Process

1. **For each day in Module 5's skeleton, generate one specific, concrete idea** within the assigned pillar — not a vague topic ("post about mindset") but a specific angle ("the one mindset shift that got me through my first losing month" — specific, personal, and immediately generative of real content).
2. **Draw directly from Module 3's recurring questions and pain points** wherever possible — a real question the audience has actually asked is a stronger, more specific idea seed than an invented topic.
3. **Check for repetition across the 30 days** — flag any two ideas that are too similar in angle even if they're in different pillars, and revise one to ensure genuine variety across the full month.
4. **Include the pillar tag, post type, and a one-line content summary** for each day — enough for Module 9 to adapt it to specific platform formats without needing to re-derive the idea.
5. **Note which days are "core" pieces for repurposing** (per Module 5's flags) and make sure those specific ideas are substantial enough to actually support being cut into multiple derivative pieces.

## Output format

```
CONTENT IDEAS (30 days):

DAY 1 — Pillar: [x] — Type: [x]
Idea: [specific, concrete one-line summary]
Hook: [from Module 6]
Lead: [from Module 7]

DAY 2 — Pillar: [x] — Type: [x]
Idea: [...]
Hook: [...]
Lead: [...]

(repeat for all 30 days)

REPETITION CHECK: [any two ideas flagged as too similar, and how they were revised]
```

## Handoff

This is the content backbone Module 9 adapts per platform, Module 10 layers proof into where relevant, Module 11 adds CTAs to, and Module 12 uses to design the repurposing system.
