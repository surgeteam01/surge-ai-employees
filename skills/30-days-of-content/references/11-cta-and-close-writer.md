# Module 11: CTA & Close Writer

**Purpose:** Write the engagement and conversion calls to action for each post — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`, adapted here to the much lighter-touch CTAs content needs compared to a sales page or email.

## Process

1. **Match CTA weight to post type** (from Module 4): educational and engagement posts get soft CTAs (a comment prompt, a save-worthy note, a share invitation, a follow-up question); authority/proof posts can carry a slightly firmer CTA (a link-in-bio mention, a DM invitation); promotional posts (the smaller share of the mix) carry the direct offer CTA.
2. **Write CTA copy that's specific**, not generic — "comment your biggest struggle with X below" beats "let me know what you think," and gives the post a real engagement mechanism rather than an afterthought line.
3. **Vary CTA type across the 30 days** — repeating "link in bio" or "comment below" on every single post reads as formulaic; mix comment prompts, saves, shares, DMs, and the occasional direct offer CTA.
4. **Never let every post pitch the same intensity** — per Module 4's content-mix ratio, only the smaller promotional share of days should carry a direct sales CTA; the rest should stay focused on engagement/value.

## Output format

```
DAY [n] — Post type: [x]
CTA: [specific engagement or conversion prompt matched to post type]
```
