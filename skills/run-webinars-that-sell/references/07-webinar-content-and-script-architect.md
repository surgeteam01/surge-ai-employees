# Module 7: Webinar Content & Script Architect

**Purpose:** Write the spoken content portion of the webinar — the open, the teaching, and the transition into the pitch. This is the module with no direct equivalent anywhere else in this repo: it has to build genuine teaching value and earned authority in real time, live, before a single word of selling happens.

## Process

1. **Write the open (first 2-5 minutes)**: a hook that mirrors the registration promise (Module 5) and the audience's top pain (Module 3), a brief credibility statement (why listen to this presenter specifically, kept short — this isn't the place for a long bio), and a clear preview of what's covered (sets expectations and gives early-leavers a reason to stay for the specific part they came for).
2. **Choose a content structure that builds toward the offer's mechanism**, not generic tips disconnected from what's being sold. A reliable pattern: teach 2-4 core concepts/steps that are genuinely useful on their own, each one revealing a piece of *why* the full method works, without handing over the complete step-by-step execution (that's what the paid offer provides) — the content should make the audience believe the result is achievable and understand the shape of how, while the offer remains the fastest/most complete path to actually doing it.
3. **Weave proof in during the content**, not just at the pitch — a relevant result or example after each core concept keeps belief building throughout rather than asking the audience to take everything on faith until the pitch arrives (use Module 8 once available; for this pass, mark placement and swap in the actual proof after Module 8 runs).
4. **Write the transition to the pitch deliberately** — this is the single highest-leverage moment in the whole funnel. A clean transition acknowledges that everything just taught works, but doing it alone takes significant time/trial-and-error, and previews that there's a faster path for those who want it — then hands off cleanly to Module 11. An abrupt "okay now let me tell you about my program" transition is the most common way a well-taught webinar still under-converts.
5. **Time-box each section** against Module 4's allocation, and flag if the content as written runs long — a webinar that teaches well but eats into pitch time by running over converts worse than one that respects the planned structure.

## Output format

```
OPEN (target: [x min])
Script/talking points: [hook, credibility, content preview]

CONTENT SECTION 1: [core concept] (target: [x min])
Script/talking points: [teaching content]
Proof placement: [marker — filled in once Module 8 runs]

CONTENT SECTION 2: [core concept] (target: [x min])
Script/talking points: [teaching content]
Proof placement: [marker]

(repeat per core concept, 2-4 total)

TRANSITION TO PITCH (target: [x min])
Script/talking points: [acknowledge what was taught works → preview the faster path → hand off to pitch]
```

## Handoff

The transition's final lines are the direct handoff into Module 11 (Pitch & Close Script Writer) — Module 11 should open by picking up exactly where this transition leaves off, not restart the persuasive arc from scratch. Proof placement markers get filled by Module 8's output once available; objection-prone moments noticed while writing content should be flagged for Module 9.
