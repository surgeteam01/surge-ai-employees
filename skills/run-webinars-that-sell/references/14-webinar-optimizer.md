# Module 14: Webinar Optimizer

**Purpose:** Score the finished webinar funnel, improve the highest-leverage moments, and produce A/B variants — the final quality gate before the webinar is delivered live or published as evergreen content.

## Process

1. **Score the assembled funnel** on a simple rubric, 1-5 each:
   - **Registration appeal** — is the title/registration page clear and specific enough to earn a sign-up?
   - **Show-up design** — does the reminder sequence do real work to convert registrations into attendance, or is it a bare minimum?
   - **Teaching value** — does the content section deliver genuine, standalone value, or does it feel like a thin excuse to reach the pitch?
   - **Transition strength** — is the content-to-pitch handoff smooth and earned, or abrupt?
   - **Proof density** — is proof placed at the content sections and the pitch, or concentrated/missing?
   - **Objection coverage** — are the top 1-2 real objections pre-empted in the pitch, not just left for Q&A?
   - **Close strength** — is the final instruction clear, specific, and low-friction?
2. **Flag the lowest-scoring 1-2 dimensions** and rewrite those sections rather than doing a generic full-funnel polish — targeted fixes beat diffuse ones, and the transition (Module 7 → 11) deserves special scrutiny given how much leverage it carries.
3. **Check script pacing and readability for live delivery**: sentence length suited to being spoken (shorter, more natural pauses than written page copy), and whether the section headers alone would let the presenter navigate the script live without losing their place.
4. **Produce at least one full A/B variant** — typically an alternate registration title + an alternate close/urgency framing, since those two elements have the highest leverage-per-word-changed (one drives registration volume, the other drives close rate on the night). Note what hypothesis each variant tests.

## Output format

```
SCORES (1-5): Registration Appeal:_ Show-Up Design:_ Teaching Value:_ Transition Strength:_ Proof Density:_ Objection Coverage:_ Close Strength:_

TOP FIXES MADE: [list, tied to lowest scores]

A/B VARIANT:
- Registration title: [alternate]
- Close/urgency framing: [alternate]
- Hypothesis being tested: [one line]
```

## Handoff

This is the last stage — present the final scored funnel plus the A/B variant to the user as the finished deliverable, ready to schedule or record. If any score is 3 or below, say so plainly rather than presenting the funnel as "done" — flag it as a known weak point and what it would take to fix (e.g. "Transition strength scored low because the pitch still opens abruptly — recommend rewriting the last 2 minutes of Module 7's content section before delivering live").
