# Module 14: Channel Optimizer

**Purpose:** Score the finished content plan on CTR potential, retention design, and SEO completeness, and produce A/B variants — the final quality gate before production begins.

## Process

1. **Score the assembled plan** on a simple rubric, 1-5 each:
   - **CTR potential** — do the title/thumbnail pairs create genuine, resolvable curiosity, or are they generic/forgettable?
   - **Packaging-to-payoff consistency** — does each script genuinely deliver on what its packaging promised?
   - **Retention structure** — are pattern interrupts, open loops, and pacing changes actually placed at realistic drop-off risk points, or is the script one long unbroken stretch?
   - **SEO completeness** — does each video have real search-intent-matched metadata, or generic tags/descriptions?
   - **Series cohesion** — do videos genuinely connect into watchable playlists, or is each video an island?
   - **Sustainability** — does the upload cadence and script complexity actually fit Module 1's production capacity?
2. **Flag the lowest-scoring 1-2 dimensions** and revise those specific videos/modules rather than a generic pass over the whole plan.
3. **Check specifically for any packaging/payoff mismatch** — flagged as the single most damaging inconsistency this pipeline can produce, since it directly hurts average-view-duration and audience trust simultaneously.
4. **Produce at least one A/B variant** — typically an alternate title/thumbnail pairing for the plan's highest-potential video (often the playlist entry-point video from Module 12), since packaging has the highest leverage-per-change of anything in the plan.

## Output format

```
SCORES (1-5): CTR Potential:_ Packaging-to-Payoff Consistency:_ Retention Structure:_ SEO Completeness:_ Series Cohesion:_ Sustainability:_

TOP FIXES MADE: [list, tied to lowest scores]

A/B VARIANT (on entry-point video):
- Alternate title/thumbnail: [detail]
- Hypothesis being tested: [one line]
```

## Handoff

This is the last stage — present the final scored content plan to the user as the finished deliverable, ready for production. If any score is 3 or below, say so plainly rather than presenting the plan as "done" — flag it as a known weak point (e.g. "Retention Structure scored low on Video 3 because there's a 4-minute unbroken talking-head stretch with no pacing change — recommend adding a pattern interrupt or b-roll cut before filming").
