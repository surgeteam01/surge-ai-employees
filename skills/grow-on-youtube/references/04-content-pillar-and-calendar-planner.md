# Module 4: Content Pillar & Calendar Planner

**Purpose:** Define recurring content pillars/series and an upload calendar — adapted directly from `30-days-of-content`'s Content Pillar & Theme Architect and Content Calendar & Cadence Planner modules, since the underlying logic (recurring themes, a cadence-matched schedule, variety across the calendar) is identical; only the unit of content (a video instead of a social post) changes.

## Process

1. **Define 3-5 content pillars/series** connecting to Module 2's transformation and Module 3's top pains/desires/search intents — on YouTube specifically, a recognizable recurring series format (e.g. a consistent title pattern, a consistent video structure) helps train the algorithm and the audience to recognize new uploads from this channel quickly.
2. **Set a realistic upload cadence** from Module 1's production capacity — YouTube rewards consistency more than raw frequency; a sustainable weekly upload reliably outperforms an unsustainable daily cadence that collapses after a month.
3. **Distribute pillars across the calendar** for variety, same principle as `30-days-of-content` — avoid publishing the same pillar/format back to back unless it's a deliberate multi-part series.
4. **Note if Shorts are in scope** (from Module 1) and, if so, plan a distinct cadence for Shorts alongside long-form, since the two often serve different jobs (Shorts for discovery/reach, long-form for depth and monetized watch-time) and shouldn't be forced into one undifferentiated calendar.

## Output format

```
CONTENT PILLARS/SERIES:
1. [Pillar/series name] — job: [what it does for the viewer] — connects to: [transformation/pain/search intent]
(3-5 total)

UPLOAD CALENDAR:
[Long-form cadence]: [frequency]
[Shorts cadence, if in scope]: [frequency]

Week 1: [pillar/series assignment per upload]
Week 2: [...]
(repeat for planning horizon, e.g. 4-8 weeks)
```
