# Module 6: Title & Thumbnail Packaging Lab

**Purpose:** Design the title/thumbnail pair for each video — this is the single highest-leverage, most YouTube-specific mechanic in the entire pipeline, since the title and thumbnail together are what decide whether a video gets clicked at all, before anything in the script matters.

## Process

1. **Generate 4-6 title options per video**, drawing on the idea's premise (Module 5) and the exact-phrase bank (Module 3), using formulas suited to YouTube specifically: a curiosity gap that's genuinely resolved by the video (not clickbait relative to actual content — a resolved curiosity gap builds trust and return viewership; an unresolved one tanks retention and long-term channel trust), a specific numbered/outcome-based title ("the 3 things that actually moved the needle"), a direct how-to/search-intent title mirroring real search phrasing, or a bold, specific claim.
2. **Design the thumbnail concept to pair with, not repeat, the title** — the strongest packaging has the title and thumbnail doing different jobs that combine into one complete curiosity gap (e.g. the title states the topic, the thumbnail shows a specific visual result or reaction that the title doesn't spell out). A thumbnail that just repeats the title in text adds little.
3. **Keep thumbnail text (if any) extremely short** — 3-5 words maximum, readable at a small size on a phone screen; the visual (a face, an object, a before/after) usually does more work than text.
4. **Score each title/thumbnail pairing** on: Curiosity (does it create a genuine, resolvable gap?), Clarity (is it obvious enough what the video is about that the right audience self-selects?), Honesty (does it accurately represent what's actually in the video — a mismatch here damages average-view-duration and channel trust even if it wins the initial click), and Visual scannability (would the thumbnail still read clearly at a small size, next to competing thumbnails)?
5. **Pick the top pairing** and note why it wins.

## Output format

```
VIDEO: [idea from Module 5]

TITLE OPTIONS (scored 1-5 on Curiosity/Clarity/Honesty/Scannability):
1. [title] — Cu:_ Cl:_ H:_ Sc:_
2. ...

TOP TITLE: [title]
THUMBNAIL CONCEPT: [visual description + any short text overlay, 3-5 words max]
Why this pairing wins: [one or two sentences]
```

## Handoff

The finalized title and premise carry forward into Module 7 (the script's payoff must genuinely deliver on what the title/thumbnail promised) and Module 9 (title language often doubles as primary SEO keyword targeting).
