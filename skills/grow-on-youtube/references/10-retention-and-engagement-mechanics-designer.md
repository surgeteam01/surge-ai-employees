# Module 10: Retention & Engagement Mechanics Designer

**Purpose:** Design the specific in-video mechanics that keep viewers watching and prompt engagement signals (likes, comments, shares) the algorithm also weighs — layered onto Module 7's script structure, not a separate piece of content.

## Process

1. **Design pattern interrupts** at the intervals flagged in Module 7's pacing notes — a visual change, a change in camera angle or energy, a graphic/text overlay, or a brief tangent that gets resolved (an "open loop") to pull the viewer through a potential drop-off point.
2. **Design at least one open loop** — a question or teaser planted early ("I'll show you exactly why in a second, but first...") that gets resolved later in the video, since unresolved curiosity is one of the most reliable retention mechanics available.
3. **Design engagement prompts placed for minimal retention cost** — a comment prompt or like/subscribe ask placed at a natural pause point (not interrupting the strongest content moment), and phrased specifically rather than generically ("comment which of these three you're trying first" beats "let me know what you think").
4. **Avoid over-stacking engagement asks** — one well-placed, specific prompt per video usually outperforms several generic asks scattered throughout, which can read as desperate and actually hurt trust/retention.

## Output format

```
PATTERN INTERRUPTS: [list, tied to Module 7's timing markers]

OPEN LOOP: [planted early] → [resolved later — timing]

ENGAGEMENT PROMPT: [specific ask] — placement: [natural pause point, not mid-peak-content]
```

## Handoff

These mechanics get woven into Module 13's final assembled script at the exact points specified — not appended as a generic list at the end.
