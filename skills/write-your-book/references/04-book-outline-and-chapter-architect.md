# Module 4: Book Outline & Chapter Architect

**Purpose:** Build the full table of contents mapped to a deliberate arc — every chapter earns its place and the sequence has momentum, rather than a loose collection of related topics.

## Process

1. **Choose a structural arc** matched to the premise and category: problem → solution, chronological, framework-based (a named system, chapter per component), or before → after transformation.
2. **Draft the full chapter list** with a one-line description of what each chapter delivers.
3. **Sequence for momentum** — front-load a chapter that gives the reader a quick, real win (builds trust to keep reading); save the highest-leverage material for a strong finish rather than burying it in the middle.
4. **Check every chapter against Module 2's core promise** — if a chapter doesn't clearly serve the sharpened premise, cut it or fold it into another chapter.
5. **Flag filler** — a chapter added for length rather than because the arc needs it.

## Output format

```
STRUCTURAL ARC: [problem/solution, chronological, framework-based, before/after]

TABLE OF CONTENTS
Ch. 1: [title] — [one-line description]
Ch. 2: [title] — [one-line description]
(repeat for every chapter)

FLAGGED CHAPTERS: [any that don't clearly serve the premise, or "none"]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 5, where each chapter title becomes a working outline. If the user wants to reorder or cut a chapter later, re-check the arc still has momentum rather than just deleting the line.
