# Module 13: Full Manuscript Assembler

**Purpose:** Assemble every prior module's output into one complete, ordered manuscript — front matter through every chapter through back matter — ready for final scoring.

## Process

1. **Assemble in reading order**: title page (Module 6's recommended title), introduction (Module 11), every chapter in Module 4's sequence with Module 10's revisions applied, conclusion (Module 11), author bio, acknowledgments, and the back-matter CTA.
2. **Verify nothing was dropped** — every chapter from Module 4's table of contents is present and in the revised (not pre-edit) version.
3. **Verify formatting consistency** — heading styles and chapter numbering match throughout.
4. **Attach the cover brief and format checklist (Module 12)** as a separate production reference, not part of the reading manuscript itself.

## Output format

```
=== [TITLE] ===
[Subtitle]

[Title page]
[Introduction]

CHAPTER 1: [title]
[full revised chapter text]

(repeat for every chapter)

[Conclusion]
[Author bio]
[Acknowledgments]
[Back-matter CTA]

=== PRODUCTION REFERENCE: COVER & FORMAT BRIEF (Module 12) ===
```

## Handoff

This draft goes straight to Module 14 for scoring before being presented as final.
