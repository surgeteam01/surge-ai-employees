# Module 14: Book Optimizer

**Purpose:** Score the finished manuscript on readability, hook strength, and completion likelihood — for nonfiction, whether readers finish matters more than whether they start — and route the finished book to its actual next step.

## Process

1. **Score the assembled manuscript** on a simple rubric, 1-5 each:
   - **Title/hook strength** — does the title and intro genuinely earn a stranger's attention?
   - **Structural clarity** — does the table of contents read as an obvious, logical progression?
   - **Promise delivery** — does the book actually deliver what the title and Module 2's premise promised, chapter by chapter?
   - **Voice consistency** — does it read as one consistent voice throughout, not a patchwork?
   - **Proof density** — is every major claim backed by something concrete from Module 9?
   - **Completion likelihood** — do pacing, chapter length variety, and momentum make it likely a reader finishes rather than stalls?
2. **Flag the lowest-scoring 1-2 dimensions** and revise those specific chapters or sections.
3. **Recommend the next step per Module 1's stated purpose** — a lead magnet routes to `build-your-email-funnel`'s welcome sequence for the readers it captures; a paid book hands off entirely to `launch-a-digital-product`'s pipeline for the launch itself.

## Output format

```
SCORES (1-5): Title/Hook Strength:_ Structural Clarity:_ Promise Delivery:_ Voice Consistency:_ Proof Density:_ Completion Likelihood:_

TOP FIXES MADE: [list, tied to lowest scores]

NEXT STEP: [build-your-email-funnel welcome sequence / launch-a-digital-product pipeline] — [why, per Module 1's purpose]
```

## Handoff

This is the last stage — present the final scored manuscript and the recommended next step as the finished deliverable. Flag any score of 3 or below plainly rather than presenting the manuscript as "done."
