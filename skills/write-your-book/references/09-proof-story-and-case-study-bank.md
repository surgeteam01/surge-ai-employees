# Module 9: Proof, Story & Case Study Bank

**Purpose:** Weave real proof — examples, case studies, data, personal stories — into the drafted chapters, since a book of pure assertion reads as opinion, not authority.

## Process

1. **Inventory available proof**: client/customer results, personal experience and stories, research or data, third-party examples.
2. **Map each piece of proof to the specific chapter(s) and claim(s)** it best supports, using the proof/example slots placed in Module 5's outlines.
3. **Flag any chapter making a claim with no proof attached** and either source proof for it or soften the claim rather than leaving it unsupported.
4. **Favor specificity** — named, dated, numbered proof over generic references ("one client saw a 40% increase in three months" beats "clients have seen great results").

## Output format

```
PROOF INVENTORY: [list of available proof by type]

PROOF-TO-CHAPTER MAP
Ch. [N]: [claim] → [specific proof used]
(repeat per chapter)

UNPROVEN CLAIMS FLAGGED: [claim + chapter, or "none"]
```

## Handoff

Feeds back into Module 8's chapter drafts (proof gets written directly into the prose) and forward into Module 10's edit pass, which checks that flagged claims got resolved.
