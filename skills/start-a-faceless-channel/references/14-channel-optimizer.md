# Module 14: Channel Optimizer

**Purpose:** Score the finished plan on CTR potential, retention design, brand consistency, and SEO completeness — the final quality gate before production begins, adapted from `grow-on-youtube`'s optimizer with an added brand-consistency dimension specific to a faceless channel.

## Process

1. **Score the assembled plan** on a simple rubric, 1-5 each:
   - **CTR potential** — do the title/thumbnail pairs create genuine curiosity without relying on a face to draw the eye?
   - **Packaging-to-payoff consistency** — does the script genuinely deliver on the packaging's promise?
   - **Retention structure** — are visual variety and pacing genuinely placed at realistic drop-off risk points?
   - **Brand consistency** — does the visual/voice/narration-personality identity (Module 12) actually feel consistent across the sampled videos, or does each video feel like a different channel made it?
   - **SEO completeness** — real search-intent-matched metadata per video?
   - **Production realism** — does the visual sourcing and voice plan actually fit Module 1's budget/capacity?
2. **Flag the lowest-scoring 1-2 dimensions** and revise those specific modules.
3. **Check specifically for brand-identity drift** across videos — this is the dimension most likely to slip in a faceless channel, since there's no live presenter naturally enforcing consistency the way a recurring face would.
4. **Produce at least one A/B variant** — typically an alternate title/thumbnail pairing for the plan's highest-potential video.

## Output format

```
SCORES (1-5): CTR Potential:_ Packaging-to-Payoff Consistency:_ Retention Structure:_ Brand Consistency:_ SEO Completeness:_ Production Realism:_

TOP FIXES MADE: [list, tied to lowest scores]

A/B VARIANT:
- Alternate title/thumbnail: [detail]
- Hypothesis being tested: [one line]
```

## Handoff

Present the final scored plan to the user as the finished deliverable, ready for production. Flag any score of 3 or below plainly rather than presenting the plan as "done."
