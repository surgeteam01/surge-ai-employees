# Module 6: Title & Thumbnail Packaging Lab

**Purpose:** Design the title/thumbnail pair for each video — reused directly from `grow-on-youtube`'s equivalent module, with one added emphasis: packaging carries even more of the click-through burden here, since there's no recognizable presenter face building a returning-viewer habit the way personality-driven channels benefit from.

## Process

1. **Generate 4-6 title options per video**, drawing on the premise (Module 5) and exact-phrase bank (Module 3): curiosity gap, numbered/outcome-based, direct how-to/search-intent mirror, or bold specific claim.
2. **Design the thumbnail concept without relying on a presenter's face or expression** — lean on strong compositional visuals instead: a striking image related to the topic, bold short text (3-5 words), high-contrast color choices, and a clear single focal point, since faceless thumbnails need to work harder compositionally to stand out without a human face (which the eye is naturally drawn to in a thumbnail grid).
3. **Score each pairing** on Curiosity, Clarity, Honesty, and Visual scannability, same rubric as `grow-on-youtube`.
4. **Pick the top pairing.**

## Output format

```
VIDEO: [from Module 5]
TITLE OPTIONS (scored 1-5): [...]
TOP TITLE: [...]
THUMBNAIL CONCEPT (no face — composition, color, short text): [...]
```
