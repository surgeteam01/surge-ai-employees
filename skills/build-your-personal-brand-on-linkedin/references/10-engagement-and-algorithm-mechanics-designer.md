# Module 10: Engagement & Algorithm Mechanics Designer

**Purpose:** Design the specific mechanics LinkedIn's algorithm and format reward beyond just the post content itself — a genuinely new mechanic in this repo, since it deals with a platform-native distribution algorithm directly.

## Process

1. **Plan "golden hour" engagement** — the first 30-60 minutes after posting disproportionately determine total reach; recommend replying to every comment quickly during this window, and if relevant, having a small number of engaged connections primed to comment early (never fake/bought engagement, only genuine relationship-based early interaction).
2. **Design comment-reply behavior** — thoughtful replies that extend the conversation (not just "thanks!") both re-signal engagement to the algorithm and build genuine relationship depth with commenters.
3. **Handle external links via the "link in comments" convention** (per Module 8) — post the link as the first comment immediately after publishing, not edited in later.
4. **Recommend a realistic engagement schedule** — checking and replying to comments across the day, not just once, since sustained engagement over several hours also feeds distribution.

## Output format

```
GOLDEN-HOUR PLAN: [what to do in the first 30-60 minutes after posting]
COMMENT-REPLY APPROACH: [depth/tone of replies]
LINK HANDLING: [confirm "link in first comment" workflow]
ENGAGEMENT SCHEDULE: [realistic check-in cadence across the day]
```

## Handoff

This is a standing operational practice, included in Module 13's assembly as a companion to the content calendar, not a one-time output.
