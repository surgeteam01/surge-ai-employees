# Module 14: LinkedIn Brand Optimizer

**Purpose:** Score the finished plan on engagement potential, profile conversion, and consistency, and produce A/B variants — the final quality gate before publishing begins.

## Process

1. **Score the assembled plan** on a simple rubric, 1-5 each:
   - **Hook strength** — do the hooks survive truncation as complete, curiosity-inducing thoughts?
   - **Profile conversion** — would a visitor arriving from a post immediately understand the value proposition and know what to do next?
   - **Pillar variety** — are pillars genuinely spread across the calendar?
   - **Format-native fit** — does formatting follow LinkedIn's actual conventions (short lines, no in-body links)?
   - **Engagement realism** — is the golden-hour/engagement-out practice actually sustainable given Module 1's time capacity?
   - **Personal specificity** — do posts read as one real person's specific experience, or generic advice?
2. **Flag the lowest-scoring 1-2 dimensions** and revise those specific modules.
3. **Produce at least one A/B variant** — typically an alternate headline (Module 4) or an alternate hook for the calendar's strongest post, since both carry outsized leverage.

## Output format

```
SCORES (1-5): Hook Strength:_ Profile Conversion:_ Pillar Variety:_ Format-Native Fit:_ Engagement Realism:_ Personal Specificity:_

TOP FIXES MADE: [list, tied to lowest scores]

A/B VARIANT:
- Alternate headline or hook: [detail]
- Hypothesis being tested: [one line]
```

## Handoff

Present the final scored plan to the user as the finished deliverable. If any score is 3 or below, flag it plainly rather than presenting the plan as "done."
