# Module 9: Proof & Testimonial Engine

**Purpose:** Turn results and testimonials into proof-based posts — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`.

## Process

1. **Categorize available proof**: results/case studies, social proof, authority proof, peer proof.
2. **Extract the specific outcome** from each raw testimonial without inventing anything.
3. **Write each as a full, LinkedIn-native post** (per Module 8's format conventions) rather than a bare quote — a results post tells the story behind the number, not just the number.
4. **Match proof to the calendar's proof-pillar slots** from Module 5.

## Output format

```
PROOF-BASED POSTS:
POST [n]
Proof type: [Result/Social/Authority/Peer]
Full post: [content-ready, LinkedIn-formatted]

GAPS: [what's missing — flag for the user to go collect]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 13's assembly at the proof-pillar slots.
