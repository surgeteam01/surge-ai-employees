# Module 11: Networking & Engagement-Out Strategy

**Purpose:** Design a strategy for engaging with other people's content and building direct relationships — a personal brand built purely on broadcasting (posting) without any "engagement-out" misses much of what actually drives LinkedIn growth and real opportunity, which is often relationship-driven rather than reach-driven.

## Process

1. **Identify a target list of accounts to engage with regularly** — peers, potential clients/employers, and slightly larger accounts in the same space whose audience overlaps with Module 3's target audience.
2. **Design a genuine commenting practice** — thoughtful, substantive comments on others' posts (not generic "great post!" comments, which build no real recognition or relationship) on a realistic regular cadence.
3. **Design a light DM/connection strategy** for warm engagement — reaching out to people who've engaged meaningfully with the user's own content, or connecting with a genuine, specific reason rather than a generic connection request. For anything resembling cold outreach at volume, point to `land-clients-with-cold-outreach` rather than duplicating that skill's sequencing logic here.
4. **Set a realistic weekly time budget** for engagement-out activity, balanced against Module 1's posting capacity — both matter, and one shouldn't fully crowd out the other.

## Output format

```
TARGET ENGAGEMENT LIST: [peer accounts, potential clients/employers, larger relevant accounts]
COMMENTING PRACTICE: [cadence + approach to substantive, non-generic comments]
DM/CONNECTION STRATEGY: [light, relationship-based — reference land-clients-with-cold-outreach for anything at volume]
WEEKLY TIME BUDGET: [split between posting and engaging-out]
```
