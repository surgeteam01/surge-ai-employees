# Module 12: CTA & Close Writer

**Purpose:** Write the calls to action for every email in the funnel and the closing lines for each sequence — with urgency that is honest, not manufactured, and CTA copy specific enough that clicking it never feels like a leap.

## Process

1. **Write CTA copy that states the next step or the outcome**, not a generic "Click Here" or "Buy Now" — e.g. "Start My 6-Week Plan" beats "Buy Now," and "Grab the free template" beats "Download."
2. **Match CTA weight to the email's job**: Welcome and Nurture emails get soft CTAs (reply, click to read, try one small action) since the ask should stay proportional to the value given so far; Sales sequence emails get a clear, singular hard CTA per email — don't split attention across two competing asks in one email.
3. **Vary CTA copy across the sequence** rather than repeating the identical line in every Sales email — each repetition is a chance to reinforce a different angle (the outcome, the risk-reversal from the guarantee, the urgency from Module 11) rather than reading as a copy-pasted button.
4. **Only use urgency/scarcity that is real**, consistent with Module 11 — never fabricate false scarcity in a CTA line just because it's a persuasive lever. If a CTA references a deadline, that deadline must be the same real one confirmed in Module 11.
5. **Write a short closing line for each sequence** (not just the funnel's final email) — Welcome closes by setting up Nurture, Nurture closes by setting up Sales, Sales closes by setting up Cart-Close, and Cart-Close closes the whole funnel with a clear final statement of what happens next (purchase confirmation flow, or a re-engagement/nurture loop for non-buyers).

## Output format

```
CTA VARIANTS (by sequence and email position):
- Welcome/Nurture (soft): [copy per email or a representative set]
- Sales #1 / #2 / #3 / Pricing: [copy per email]
- Cart-Close (by position): [copy per email]

URGENCY ELEMENT USED IN CTAs: [real element from Module 11, or "none available — recommend not fabricating one"]

SEQUENCE CLOSING LINES:
Welcome → Nurture: [one line]
Nurture → Sales: [one line]
Sales → Cart-Close: [one line]
Cart-Close → funnel end: [one line, covering both buyer and non-buyer paths]
```

## Handoff

Feeds directly into Module 13 for placement inside each assembled email, and Module 14 will test whether the CTAs and closes are strong enough across the funnel or need an A/B variant.
