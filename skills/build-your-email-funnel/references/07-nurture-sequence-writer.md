# Module 7: Nurture Sequence Writer

**Purpose:** Deliver real, standalone value across a sequence of emails while planting the seeds for the eventual offer — so that by the time the Sales sequence arrives, the reader already half-believes the transformation is possible and already trusts this sender to deliver it.

## Process

1. **Confirm sequence length and cadence from Module 4's map.** Awareness stage decides how much runway this sequence needs: unaware/problem-aware audiences need more nurture emails to build category belief before any pitch would land; product-aware/most-aware audiences need less, or this sequence can be shortened significantly.
2. **Pick a value theme per email**, each teaching one specific, useful thing related to the transformation (Module 2) — not a random content calendar. Every email should answer a real question or solve a small piece of the reader's problem in isolation, so it's worth reading even if the reader never buys anything.
3. **Use the value-stack mindset from offer design, applied per email**: each email is itself a "component" with a value the reader can feel — pick the teaching points that most directly preview the paid offer's actual mechanism, so the free value and the paid offer feel like the same throughline, not two disconnected things.
4. **Plant proof and objection-handling lightly, not as a pitch.** Where natural, weave in a brief result, a quick client story, or a preemptive answer to a common doubt (using Module 8 and Module 9's output once available) — but keep the ratio heavily weighted toward value; a nurture email that pitches is not a nurture email.
5. **End every email with a soft CTA**, not a sales CTA: reply with a question, click through to read more, try a small action related to the lesson. Save the hard CTA for Module 10.
6. **Write the last Nurture email as a bridge**, explicitly signaling that the free value is ending and something is coming — this is the direct handoff into the Sales sequence, so the reader isn't blindsided by the shift in tone.

## Output format

```
NURTURE SEQUENCE ([count] emails, cadence: [spacing]):

EMAIL 1 — [value theme]
Body: [full email copy]
Soft CTA: [reply/click/small action]

EMAIL 2 — [value theme]
Body: [full email copy]
Soft CTA: [reply/click/small action]

(repeat per email)

BRIDGE EMAIL (last in sequence) — Signals the pitch is coming
Body: [full email copy]
```

## Handoff

The teaching points used here should be the same ones the Sales sequence (Module 10) references back to — the pitch should feel like "the thing I've been showing you the pieces of," not a new topic. If proof or objection-handling was woven in lightly, flag which pieces of Module 8 and Module 9's output were used here so Module 13 doesn't duplicate them verbatim later in the Sales sequence.
