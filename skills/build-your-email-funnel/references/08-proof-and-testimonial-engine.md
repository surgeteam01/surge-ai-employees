# Module 8: Proof & Testimonial Engine

**Purpose:** Turn raw results and rough testimonials into persuasive, specific proof blocks matched to specific emails in the funnel. Vague praise ("great course, loved it!") does almost nothing — specific, outcome-focused proof does the heavy lifting, and unlike a sales page, a funnel gets to spread proof across several emails instead of cramming it into one section.

## Process

1. **Categorize available proof** into types: results/case studies (numbers, before-after), social proof (volume — "1,200 people have..."), authority proof (credentials, media, notable clients), and peer proof (testimonials from people like the reader).
2. **For each raw testimonial, extract the specific outcome** buried inside it. Rewrite for clarity and impact without inventing anything — never fabricate a result, number, or quote that wasn't given. If a testimonial is vague, either ask the user for the specific result behind it or use it only as supporting color, not as headline proof.
3. **Match proof type to email, not just to a page section**: authority/social proof works well early (Welcome sequence Email 2, or the first Sales email) to build initial trust; specific case-study proof belongs in the Sales sequence emails that recap the offer stack; peer testimonials pair best with the dedicated objection email ("will this work for someone like me?").
4. **Write proof in more than one length**: a one-line pull-quote version (fits inline in a Nurture email without breaking the value-first flow) and a fuller 2-4 sentence version (for a Sales sequence email built around proof), so Module 13 can choose based on the email.
5. **Spread proof across the funnel rather than repeating the same one or two pieces in every email** — if the proof pool is thin, say so and prioritize the strongest 2-3 pieces for the Sales sequence, using lighter/shorter proof elsewhere.

## Output format

```
PROOF BLOCKS:

[Type: Result/Authority/Social/Peer]
Short: "[one-line version]"
Full: "[2-4 sentence version]"
Best placement: [sequence + email position, e.g. "Sales #2"]

(repeat per proof piece)

GAPS: [what proof is missing that would strengthen the funnel — flag for the user to go collect]
```

## Handoff

If "none yet" was the answer in Module 1, say so plainly here rather than inventing placeholder testimonials — recommend a first proof strategy instead (e.g. a beta cohort, a founder's-story-as-proof angle used in the Welcome sequence, or a money-back guarantee in Module 10 to substitute for missing social proof). Proof placements noted here should be respected by Modules 6, 7, 10, and 13 rather than re-decided later.
