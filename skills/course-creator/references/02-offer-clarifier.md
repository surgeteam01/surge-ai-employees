# Module 2: Offer Clarifier

**Purpose:** Sharpen the offer and the transformation it delivers before designing a single lesson. A course built around a vague transformation produces a curriculum that wanders — lots of content, no clear destination the student can feel themselves approaching.

## Process

1. **State the current offer in one sentence**, as the user described it.
2. **Stress-test it against the Value Equation**: Dream Outcome (how big/specific is the result?), Likelihood of Success (how believable is it, especially for a self-directed learning product?), Time Delay (how fast do they get there?), Effort & Sacrifice (how much work does it actually take — courses in particular need to be honest here, since overselling ease is a direct driver of the "I never finished it" objection). Flag whichever of these four is weakest or missing.
3. **Rewrite the offer as a before → after transformation**, not a syllabus. Bad: "12 modules on digital marketing." Good: "go from posting randomly with no strategy to a repeatable content system that brings in 3-5 qualified leads a week, in 6 weeks of focused work."
4. **Check specificity.** Push for numbers, timeframes, and named outcomes wherever honestly possible — this specific transformation is what Module 4 designs the entire curriculum around.
5. **Flag anything unprovable or overclaimed** — this will surface again at Module 9 (Objection Handler) and, worse, as a completion/satisfaction problem if the course itself can't actually deliver what the marketing promised.

## Output format

```
CURRENT OFFER (as given): [one sentence]
WEAKEST LINK: [Dream Outcome / Likelihood / Time / Effort]
SHARPENED OFFER (one sentence): [rewrite]
TRANSFORMATION STATEMENT (one paragraph, before → after): [rewrite]
RED FLAGS: [anything overclaimed, vague, or unprovable]
```

## Handoff

The sharpened offer and transformation statement become the backbone of Module 4 (Curriculum Architect — every module/lesson should trace back to this transformation) and get passed directly into the Stage 6 handoff to `launch-a-digital-product` so its own Offer Clarifier stage doesn't re-run from scratch. If the user pushes back on the sharpened version, ask which specific part felt wrong and refine.
