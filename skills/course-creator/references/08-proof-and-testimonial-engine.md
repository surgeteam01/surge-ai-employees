# Module 8: Proof & Testimonial Engine

**Purpose:** Turn raw student results and testimonials into persuasive, specific proof blocks — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`, with an added emphasis specific to courses: proof of completion and outcome, not just satisfaction.

## Process

1. **Categorize available proof** into types: results/case studies (a student's specific before-after outcome), completion proof (a specific student who finished and what they built/achieved), social proof (volume — "200+ students have completed this"), authority proof (credentials, media), and peer proof (testimonials from students similar to the target audience).
2. **Prioritize outcome and completion proof over generic satisfaction quotes** — "I loved this course" does far less work than "I finished the 6-week program and landed my first client within a month of completing Module 4," since the completion detail itself pre-empts the "will I actually finish this" objection before it's even raised.
3. **Extract the specific outcome** from each raw testimonial without inventing anything. If vague, ask for the specific result or hold it back rather than using it as headline proof.
4. **Write proof in more than one length** for use across the launch (short pull-quote and fuller version).

## Output format

```
PROOF BLOCKS:

[Type: Result/Completion/Social/Authority/Peer]
Short: "[one-line version]"
Full: "[2-4 sentence version]"
Best placement: [pre-launch content / sales page / objection rebuttal]

(repeat per proof piece)

GAPS: [what proof is missing, especially completion-specific proof if only satisfaction quotes exist — flag for the user to go collect]
```

## Handoff

If "none yet," recommend a beta-cohort strategy to generate real completion proof before a full launch, rather than inventing placeholder testimonials. Feeds Module 6's launch handoff and Module 9's objection rebuttals.
