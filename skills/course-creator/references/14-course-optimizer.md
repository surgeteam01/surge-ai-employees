# Module 14: Course Optimizer

**Purpose:** Score the finished course package on completion risk, conversion strength, and margin — the final quality gate before content production and launch begin.

## Process

1. **Score the assembled course** on a simple rubric, 1-5 each:
   - **Curriculum-transformation alignment** — does every module clearly trace back to the sharpened transformation, or does the curriculum wander into tangential topics?
   - **Pacing realism** — is the course length and pacing realistic given Module 3's time-scarcity signals, or optimistic?
   - **Early-win strength** — does the student reach a genuine, felt result quickly, or does momentum-building get deferred too long?
   - **Completion system completeness** — does Module 12 cover onboarding, ongoing accountability, and re-engagement, or is one of the three thin/missing?
   - **Launch quality** — carry forward the score from `launch-a-digital-product`'s own optimizer (Module 6's handoff) rather than re-scoring independently.
   - **Support load realism** — does the completion system's support commitment actually hold up at the enrollment numbers the launch targets?
2. **Flag the lowest-scoring 1-2 dimensions** and revisit those specific modules — a low completion-system score is worth fixing before launch even if the marketing scores well, since weak completion directly damages the proof available for the next launch.
3. **Check the completion-objection rebuttal (Module 9) against the actual completion system (Module 12)** specifically — this is the single most common inconsistency in a first assembly pass, since the two are often written before the other is finalized.
4. **Produce at least one A/B variant** — typically an alternate pricing/tier structure (single option vs. self-study/with-coaching split) or an alternate guarantee condition, since both have outsized influence on both conversion and completion rate.

## Output format

```
SCORES (1-5): Curriculum-Transformation Alignment:_ Pacing Realism:_ Early-Win Strength:_ Completion System Completeness:_ Launch Quality:_ Support Load Realism:_

TOP FIXES MADE: [list, tied to lowest scores]

REBUTTAL-VS-SYSTEM CONSISTENCY CHECK: [does Module 9's completion rebuttal accurately reflect Module 12's actual system?]

A/B VARIANT:
- Alternate pricing/tier structure or guarantee condition: [detail]
- Hypothesis being tested: [one line]
```

## Handoff

This is the last stage — present the final scored course package to the user as the finished deliverable, ready to begin content production and launch. If any score is 3 or below, say so plainly rather than presenting the course as "done" — flag it as a known weak point (e.g. "Completion System Completeness scored low because there's no re-engagement path for stalled students defined yet — recommend fixing this before enrolling the first cohort").
