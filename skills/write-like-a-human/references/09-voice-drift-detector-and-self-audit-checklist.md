# Module 9: Voice Drift Detector & Self-Audit Checklist

**Purpose:** Build a checklist for catching voice drift over time — a real risk once a voice profile is in active use across many pieces of content, multiple writers, or repeated AI sessions, since small deviations compound and the voice can slowly genericize without anyone noticing any single piece going wrong.

## Process

1. **Identify the most likely drift patterns** for this specific voice: which attributes from Module 3 are most likely to erode first under time pressure (e.g. distinctive rhythm is often the first thing lost when someone or something is rushing), which signature phrases (Module 4) are most likely to either disappear from disuse or become overused into a tic, and which generic patterns (Module 5) are most likely to creep back in under deadline pressure.
2. **Write a short self-audit checklist** — a small number of fast, concrete questions someone (or an AI) can run against a finished piece before publishing: does this still sound like the voice summary from Module 3? Does it lean on any signature phrase too heavily, or not at all? Does it contain any pattern from Module 5's avoid list that crept back in?
3. **Recommend a periodic full recalibration cadence** — e.g. every quarter, or after a significant volume of new content, pull a handful of recent pieces and re-run them informally against Module 8's before/after samples to catch slow drift that a single-piece audit might miss.
4. **Note what to do when drift is caught** — is it a one-off piece to revise, or a sign the whole profile needs updating (e.g. the person's actual voice has genuinely evolved, which is different from accidental drift and should update Module 3's map rather than just being corrected back to an old baseline).

## Output format

```
LIKELY DRIFT PATTERNS: [attributes/phrases/generic-patterns most at risk of drifting, and why]

SELF-AUDIT CHECKLIST (fast, per-piece):
1. [Question]
2. [Question]
3. [Question]
(3-5 total, kept short enough to actually use every time)

RECALIBRATION CADENCE: [recommended frequency for a fuller review]

DRIFT VS. GENUINE EVOLUTION: [how to tell the difference, and what to do for each]
```

## Handoff

This checklist should be applied by Module 10 as a final check on every rewrite, and handed to the user as an ongoing tool independent of any single pipeline run — this is meant to be used repeatedly, long after this skill's initial run is finished.
