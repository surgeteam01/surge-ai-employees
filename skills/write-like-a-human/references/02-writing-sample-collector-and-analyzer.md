# Module 2: Writing Sample Collector & Analyzer

**Purpose:** Analyze the real writing samples from Module 1 for actual patterns — this is the evidence base every later module in this pipeline draws from, and the reason this skill insists on real samples rather than working from self-description alone.

## Process

1. **Analyze sentence-level patterns**: average sentence length and how much it varies, common sentence structures (short and punchy vs. long and layered), punctuation habits (heavy use of em-dashes, semicolons, ellipses, exclamation points, or a clean minimal style), and paragraph length.
2. **Analyze vocabulary and register**: word choice level (plain/everyday vs. technical/elevated), any consistent jargon or field-specific language, contractions (used freely vs. avoided), and how formal or casual the word choices skew.
3. **Analyze structural and rhetorical habits**: does the person tend to open with a question, a statement, a story? Do they use lists, analogies, direct address to the reader ("you"), rhetorical questions, or humor? Do they tend toward certainty and directness, or toward hedging and nuance?
4. **Analyze what's notably absent** — patterns the person consistently avoids (e.g. never uses exclamation points, never opens with a question, never hedges) are as diagnostic as what's present.
5. **Cross-check against the self-description from Module 1** — note any place the real samples contradict the self-description (e.g. described as "concise" but samples show long, exploratory sentences); flag this directly, since it's valuable information, not a contradiction to quietly resolve one way or the other.

## Output format

```
SENTENCE-LEVEL PATTERNS: [length, variation, structure tendencies, punctuation habits, paragraph length]

VOCABULARY & REGISTER: [word choice level, jargon, contractions, formality]

STRUCTURAL/RHETORICAL HABITS: [opening styles, lists, analogies, direct address, rhetorical questions, humor, certainty vs. hedging]

NOTABLE ABSENCES: [what's consistently avoided]

SELF-DESCRIPTION CROSS-CHECK: [any contradiction between how they describe their voice and what the samples actually show]
```

## Handoff

This raw analysis feeds Module 3 (distilling it into a structured attribute map), Module 4 (extracting specific recurring phrases), and Module 6 (turning patterns into concrete style rules). If sample volume was thin (per Module 1's flag), note that confidence in this analysis should be treated as provisional.
