# Module 5: AI-Tell Detector & De-Genericizer

**Purpose:** Build an explicit list of the generic, robotic, or overused patterns to actively avoid — this module exists because the most common failure mode in AI-assisted writing isn't a bad voice, it's no voice at all: a competent but generic style that could have been written by anyone, or anything.

## Process

1. **Catalog common AI-sounding tells** to check against and avoid by default, regardless of the specific voice being captured: stock openers ("In today's fast-paced world," "In the ever-evolving landscape of..."), stock transitions ("Let's dive in," "Without further ado"), excessive hedging/false balance ("On one hand... on the other hand..." used reflexively rather than when genuinely warranted), overused rhetorical structures (rule-of-three lists used in every single paragraph), generic enthusiasm ("game-changing," "revolutionary," "unlock your potential"), and an overuse of em-dashes or semicolons as a stylistic crutch rather than a genuine feature of the person's real writing (per Module 2's actual analysis).
2. **Cross-check this generic list against Module 2's real analysis** — some items on the generic-avoid list might actually be genuine, natural parts of this specific person's real voice (e.g. if they genuinely do favor em-dashes in their real samples); don't strip out something that's actually authentic to them just because it appears on a generic "AI tell" list. The test is always: does the real sample evidence support this, or is it filler that crept in from generic training patterns?
3. **Identify specific instances of genericness in any draft the user brings in** (e.g. a previous AI-generated draft that felt "off," from Module 1) and name exactly what made it feel generic, as concrete, teachable examples.
4. **Write a short, memorable "avoid by default" list** specific to this voice profile — shorter and more usable than an exhaustive catalog, since a checklist too long to actually remember doesn't get used.

## Output format

```
GENERIC/AI-SOUNDING PATTERNS TO AVOID (cross-checked against real samples):
- [Pattern] — confirmed generic (not present in real samples) / OR flagged as actually authentic to this voice, keep it

SPECIFIC EXAMPLES FROM USER'S "FELT INAUTHENTIC" DRAFT (if provided): [named, concrete instances]

SHORT AVOID-BY-DEFAULT LIST (memorable, not exhaustive): [...]
```

## Handoff

This list feeds Module 6 (style guide's "don'ts" section) and Module 10 (the rewrite engine checks every rewrite against this list specifically, not just against what to include). It's also broadly reusable as a general quality check on any AI-generated writing across every other skill in this repo.
