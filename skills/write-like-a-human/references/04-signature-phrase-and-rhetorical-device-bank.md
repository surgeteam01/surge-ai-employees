# Module 4: Signature Phrase & Rhetorical Device Bank

**Purpose:** Catalog the specific recurring phrases, analogies, and sentence patterns that are distinctly this person's — these are the concrete, reusable fingerprints of the voice, more specific and more useful in practice than the general attribute map from Module 3.

## Process

1. **Extract genuinely recurring phrases** from Module 2's samples — not one-off clever lines, but patterns that show up more than once: a particular way of transitioning between ideas, a recurring analogy or metaphor family, a favorite way of opening or closing a piece of writing, specific words used unusually often.
2. **Extract recurring rhetorical devices**: does the person favor short standalone sentences for emphasis? Rhetorical questions? A specific rule-of-three pattern? Self-deprecating asides? Direct challenges to the reader's assumptions?
3. **Distinguish signature elements from clichés or filler** — a genuinely distinctive recurring phrase is different from a generic verbal tic that doesn't add character (e.g. "honestly" used as a filler word isn't the same as a specific, quotable turn of phrase this person is known for). Only bank the former as reusable signature material.
4. **Note the natural frequency of use** — a signature phrase used once every few pieces reads as distinctive; the same phrase forced into every single piece of writing reads as a tic and undermines the naturalness this whole skill is trying to preserve. Flag a sensible reuse frequency, not "use as often as possible."

## Output format

```
SIGNATURE PHRASES: [list of specific recurring phrases/transitions/openers/closers, with a note on natural frequency]

RECURRING RHETORICAL DEVICES: [short-sentence emphasis, rhetorical questions, rule-of-three, self-deprecation, direct challenges, etc.]

DISTINGUISHED FROM FILLER: [verbal tics or generic filler noted but explicitly NOT banked as signature material]
```

## Handoff

This bank feeds Module 6 (style guide references it directly), Module 8 (before/after samples should demonstrate these devices in use), and Module 10 (the rewrite engine draws from this bank rather than inventing generic replacements). Reuse frequency notes should be respected — overusing a signature phrase is a fast way to make a captured voice start sounding like a parody of itself.
