# Module 5: Headline Lab

**Purpose:** Write and score hero headlines, pre-headlines, and subheads. The headline is the single highest-leverage sentence on the page — most readers decide whether to keep reading within seconds of it.

## Process

1. **Generate at least 8-10 headline options**, drawing on the sharpened offer (Module 2), the top desire/pain (Module 3), and multiple formulas so options are genuinely different, not just re-worded:
   - **4 U's**: Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific, Useful
   - **Benefit + Timeframe + Objection-handle**: "Get [result] in [time] without [common objection/sacrifice]"
   - **Direct promise**: state the transformation plainly, no gimmick
   - **Curiosity/question**: opens a gap the reader wants closed (use sparingly — riskier, can feel clickbait if the promise underneath is weak)
   - **Customer-language mirror**: built directly from the exact-phrase bank in Module 3
2. **Score each option** on: Clarity (can a stranger understand it in 3 seconds?), Specificity (numbers/timeframes beat vague claims), Believability (does it feel earned given the proof available?), and Relevance (does it speak to the actual audience segment?).
3. **Pick the top 2-3** and pair each with a matching pre-headline (context/qualifier above it) and subhead (adds the specificity or objection-handle the headline didn't have room for).
4. **Cast the winner into the blueprint's hero stack.** Block 1 of `00-page-blueprint.md` is not one headline — it is three stacked lines, and the third is the one most pages leave out:

   ```
   [Biggest, most desirable outcome]
   [In a specific timeframe]
   [Without pain 1, pain 2, pain 3]
   ```

   The three pains come straight from Module 3's pain list, in the buyer's own words, and there are **three** of them — one reads as a caveat, three read as someone who has actually met this buyer. If the offer has no honest timeframe, say so and drop line 2 rather than inventing one; the "without" line is the harder one to lose.

## Output format

```
HEADLINE OPTIONS (scored 1-5 on Clarity / Specificity / Believability / Relevance):
1. [headline] — C:_ S:_ B:_ R:_
2. ...

TOP PICK: [headline]
Pre-headline: [line above]
Subhead: [line below]
Why this wins: [one or two sentences]

HERO STACK (blueprint block 1):
Line 1 (outcome): [...]
Line 2 (timeframe): [...]
Line 3 (without): without [pain 1], [pain 2], or [pain 3]
```

## Handoff

The top pick's language should echo forward into Module 6 (Lead Writer) so the transition from headline to opening paragraph feels seamless, not like two different writers.
