# Module 12: CTA & Close Writer

**Purpose:** Write the calls to action throughout the page and the final closing copy — with urgency that is honest, not manufactured.

## Process

1. **Write button/CTA copy that states the next step or the outcome**, not a generic "Submit" or "Buy Now" — e.g. "Start My 6-Week Plan" beats "Buy Now."
2. **There are five placements, not one.** `00-page-blueprint.md` puts the CTA in the header bar and then at blocks 5, 10, 13 and 16. Module 11 builds the block; you write the **line above each placement**, because that line is the only thing that changes:

   | Placement | What the reader has just read | So the line leans on |
   |---|---|---|
   | Header bar | Nothing | The offer name — this is a nav button, not a pitch |
   | Block 5 | The end-state promise + one testimonial | The outcome |
   | Block 10 | What's inside, the bonuses, the value stack | The value gap |
   | Block 13 | The guarantee and the FAQ | Risk reversal — there is nothing left to lose |
   | Block 16 | The founder letter | The invitation, warmly |

   Vary those lines. Do **not** vary the block itself — a reader who scrolled past the same block twice should recognise it instantly the third time, and re-styling it every placement reads as four different offers.
3. **Only use urgency/scarcity that is real** — a genuine cohort deadline, real limited capacity, an actual price increase date. Never fabricate false scarcity (fake countdown timers, "only 3 left" when untrue) — this is both an ethics line and, practically, something that damages trust and repeat business once discovered.
   Note also: the blueprint's block 8 opens "When you enrol during this special, limited time period…". That line is **conditional on a real deadline**. No cohort close, no price rise, no capacity cap → rewrite it without the urgency claim rather than leaving the template's words in place. This is the single most common way a page built on this structure ends up lying.
4. **Write the two closing blocks the blueprint asks for**, in this order — they do different jobs and the page needs both:

   **Block 14 — "Still thinking about it?"** A self-qualifying list, headed something like "You're ready to achieve [outcome] if…" or "[Offer] is perfect for you if…". Let the reader disqualify or commit *themselves*; a list they measure themselves against converts better than a paragraph telling them they are ready. Cover the range: the person just getting started who wants to do it right, the person already struggling who wants it fixed once and for all, a desirable trait backed by a **specific action** ("already spending four hours a week trying to…"), the big fear the promise addresses, and readiness to do the work. Write these as things that are true of them, not as requirements.

   **Block 15 — the founder letter.** Photo, then "I can't wait for you to join [offer]", then 1–2 paragraphs on **why you built this** — how it changed your life and how it will change theirs. Reiterate who it is for and that they are in the right place, remind them it is risk-free (point at the named guarantee), one final invitation, signature. This is the only block on the page written person-to-person rather than brand-to-market, so drop the marketing register entirely. If the user has given you no personal story, mark it `NEEDS INPUT` and ask — an invented founder motivation is the worst possible thing to fabricate on a page whose job is trust.

## Output format

```
CTA LINES (one per placement, block itself comes from Module 11):
- Header bar: [copy]
- Block 5 (after end-state + testimonial) — leans on outcome: [copy]
- Block 10 (after value stack) — leans on value gap: [copy]
- Block 13 (after guarantee + FAQ) — leans on risk reversal: [copy]
- Block 16 (after founder letter) — leans on invitation: [copy]

URGENCY ELEMENT: [real element used, or "none available — block 8 rewritten without the limited-time line"]

STILL THINKING ABOUT IT (block 14):
Headline: [...]
- [trait / situation]  (×5-7)

FOUNDER LETTER (block 15):
[1-2 paragraphs, person-to-person voice]
Signature: [name]
Image slot: [...]
```

## Handoff

Feeds directly into Module 13 for placement, and Module 14 will test whether the CTA and close are strong enough or need an A/B variant.
