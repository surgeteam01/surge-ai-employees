# Module 6: Competitor & Visual Landscape Scan

**Purpose:** Understand what competitors' brands actually look like and where the visual gap is — adapted from `nail-your-pricing`'s Competitor & Market Pricing Scan and `find-your-niche`'s Market Landscape Scan logic, applied to visual identity instead of pricing or positioning.

## Process

1. **Identify how known competitors (Module 1) present visually**: color choices, typography style, logo approach (wordmark, symbol, combination), and overall visual register (minimal, maximal, playful, corporate).
2. **Categorize the competitive visual landscape** — is everyone clustered around a similar look (a common failure mode in many categories, e.g. every SaaS company using a similar blue-and-white minimal style), or is there real variety?
3. **Identify the visual gap** — where, read through Module 3's audience research, there's room to look genuinely different from the cluster while still feeling appropriate to the category and trustworthy to the audience (standing out for its own sake, disconnected from what the audience actually trusts, isn't a real gap — it's just different).
4. **Flag if the intended personality (Module 4) would blend in or stand out** against this landscape, and whether that's the right call given the business's competitive strategy.

## Output format

```
COMPETITOR VISUAL LANDSCAPE:
[Competitor] — colors: [...] — type style: [...] — logo approach: [...] — overall register: [...]
(repeat per competitor)

CLUSTERING: [where most competitors converge visually]
VISUAL GAP IDENTIFIED: [where there's room to differentiate while staying trustworthy to the audience]
STAND-OUT VS. BLEND-IN CHECK: [does Module 4's personality profile stand out here, and is that the right call?]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 7 (color) and Module 8 (typography) directly — both should be chosen with this landscape in mind, not independently of it.
