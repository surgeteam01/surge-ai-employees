# Module 10: Visual Mood & Imagery Style Guide

**Purpose:** Define the photography, illustration, and iconography style the brand uses across marketing materials — a consistent imagery style is often what a customer notices most, even more than the logo itself, since it appears far more frequently across ads, social posts, and the website.

## Process

1. **Define photography style** (if used): lighting mood (bright/airy vs. moody/dramatic), color treatment (warm vs. cool grading, saturated vs. desaturated), subject style (candid/lifestyle vs. staged/studio), consistent with Module 4's personality and Module 7's palette.
2. **Define illustration/graphic style** (if used): flat vs. dimensional, line-art vs. filled, consistent color usage from Module 7.
3. **Define iconography style**: line weight, corner treatment (sharp vs. rounded — rounded often reads as friendlier, sharp as more precise/technical), fill vs. outline.
4. **Provide reference direction usable for AI image generation or stock photo curation** — specific enough that someone selecting or generating images could consistently apply this style rather than picking visually inconsistent images that happen to be individually nice.

## Output format

```
PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE (if used): [lighting, color treatment, subject style]
ILLUSTRATION STYLE (if used): [flat/dimensional, line style, color usage]
ICONOGRAPHY STYLE: [line weight, corner treatment, fill/outline]
AI-GENERATION/STOCK-CURATION DIRECTION: [specific enough for consistent selection/generation]
```
