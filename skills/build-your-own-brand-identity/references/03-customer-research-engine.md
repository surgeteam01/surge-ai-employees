# Module 3: Customer Research Engine

**Purpose:** Build a Voice-of-Customer profile — reused directly from `sales-page-that-converts`, adapted here toward what visually and emotionally resonates with this specific audience, since a visual identity's job is to feel right to the people it's for, not to the business owner's personal taste.

## Process

1. **Pull from real material first**: what the audience already responds to in adjacent brands, aesthetic preferences visible in community/social discussion. Ask before generating from scratch.
2. **If none exists**, build a hypothesis-based profile, labeled as such.
3. **Identify what visual register this audience trusts** — a more conservative, professional audience often responds differently to bold, saturated color and playful typography than a younger, trend-forward audience would; this isn't about the business owner's preference, it's about what builds trust with this specific buyer.
4. **Extract exact phrases** the audience uses when describing brands they love or distrust in this category — useful signal for Module 4's personality mapping.

## Output format

```
TOP PAINS/DESIRES (ranked): [...]
VISUAL REGISTER THIS AUDIENCE TRUSTS: [conservative/bold, minimal/maximal, playful/serious — reasoning]
EXACT-PHRASE BANK (how they describe brands they love/distrust): [...]
CONFIDENCE: [Real data / Hypothesis]
```

## Handoff

Feeds Module 4 (personality mapping) and Module 6 (competitor scan should be read through this audience's lens, not a generic aesthetic judgment).
