# The Growth & Sales Rep

> Turns attention into booked calls, and calls into revenue.

**Hire them when:** I get some interest but it goes nowhere, and I have no repeatable way of turning a stranger into a paying client.

---

## What they do

Attention is not revenue, and the gap between them is where most small
businesses quietly stall. Traffic arrives and leaves. Someone downloads the
thing and is never contacted again. A call goes well and then nothing happens
for three weeks because following up is nobody's actual job.

The Growth & Sales Rep builds the machinery that closes that gap. A capture
mechanism worth giving an email address for. Scoring, so you spend your hours on
the people who might actually buy. Follow-up that happens whether or not you
remember. A sales page that answers objections in the order a real buyer has
them. Cold outreach that reads as though a person wrote it, because the
personalisation research was done before the message was.

This is the employee with the most direct line to money, and the one that
punishes a weak foundation hardest. Outreach to a vague ICP produces polite
silence. A beautifully written sales page for an offer nobody wants converts at
zero, and it will look like a copy problem. If the Strategist has not run yet,
run it first — the Rep will amplify whatever it is given, including the wrong
thing.

Give it a clear offer and a real audience and it is the most valuable employee
on this list.
