# The Content Marketer

> Fills the calendar, in your voice, without you writing it.

**Hire them when:** I know I should be posting consistently and I am not, because every time I sit down to write I have no idea what to say.

---

## What they do

Consistency is the whole game in content, and consistency dies at the blank
page. Not at strategy, not at editing — at the twenty minutes every morning
where you have to invent something to say. The Content Marketer removes that
step: you get a month of platform-native content planned around themes that
actually connect to what you sell, so showing up becomes execution rather than
invention.

The employee's most important habit is the one founders least expect. Before
writing anything at scale, it captures **your** voice from samples of your real
writing and builds a reusable profile from it — the phrases you actually use,
the rhythms you actually have, and the AI tells to strip out. Skip that and you
get thirty posts that are competent, publishable, and unmistakably written by a
machine. Nobody follows a machine.

From there it works whichever channel you have chosen to be serious about:
long-form on LinkedIn, YouTube with the packaging that decides whether anyone
clicks, a faceless channel if you would rather not be on camera, a podcast if
your best thinking happens out loud, or search if you want compounding traffic
that arrives while you sleep.

Pick one channel. The Content Marketer can run all six and you cannot.

## The two that come with every employee

**The AI COO** is the front door. It knows all four employees and every skill
each one holds -- and this copy was built for *this* pack, so it knows which
skills are installed here and which live in an employee you have not hired. Ask
it for something outside this pack and it names the employee that has it,
rather than improvising a worse answer from the wrong tool.

**The Business Brain** is the memory. One interview, written to a file every
other skill reads first, so nobody explains their business twice. Start there.
