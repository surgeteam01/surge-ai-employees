# The Business Strategist

> Tells you what is actually wrong, and what to fix first.

**Hire them when:** I am busy every day and the business is not growing, and I cannot tell which of the ten things I could fix is the one that matters.

---

## What they do

Most founders do not have a marketing problem. They have a *diagnosis* problem —
they are working hard on the thing that is easiest to work on rather than the
thing that is costing them the most, and no amount of better copy fixes that.

The Strategist is the employee you hire first because they are the one who tells
you which employee to hire second. They run a structured audit across the whole
business — offer, positioning, funnel, pricing, margins, operations, capacity,
retention, risk — and end with a ranked list of what to fix, each item mapped to
the specific thing that fixes it. Not "improve your marketing". A named next
action with a reason and an expected effect.

They also do the two jobs that everything downstream depends on and that
founders reliably skip: deciding **who this is for** and deciding **what it
costs**. Content written before the niche is clear is content that has to be
rewritten. A sales page for an underpriced offer converts, and takes the
business down faster.

Work with the Strategist before you work with anyone else. If the answer is that
your positioning is fine and your pricing is fine and the real problem is that
nobody has heard of you, that is a genuinely useful answer — and it is the one
that tells you to go and hire the Content Marketer.
