# Module 3: The owner

**Purpose:** Decide which AI Employee holds the new skill. A skill nobody holds is a
skill nobody reaches for; a skill on the wrong employee is reached for by the wrong
one.

## The default is you

You made the skill because the person asked *you* for this work. So the owner is the
AI Employee you are, unless the job plainly belongs to another one. Say it as one
sentence the person can correct: "I'll keep this one — it's content work."

## Who you are

- **In Agent Studio** you know: your name is in your own instructions.
- **In the person's own Claude** you were installed as part of an AI Employee pack.
  Read `ai-coo/references/01-your-employees.md` beside this skill: it lists the
  employees in this pack. One employee there → that is you. More than one pack
  installed, or none listed → ask the person which employee they were working with.

## When it is somebody else's

Move it only when the job is clearly another employee's work, and say why in a line:

| Pack key | AI Employee | Holds work like |
|---|---|---|
| `content-marketer` | The Content Marketer | posts, calendars, voice, LinkedIn, YouTube, podcasts, SEO |
| `growth-and-sales` | The Growth & Sales Rep | leads, outreach, sales pages, email funnels, ads, webinars |
| `brand-and-product` | The Brand & Product Builder | brand identity, product photos, courses, books, digital products, build help |
| `business-strategist` | The Business Strategist | analysis, audits, niche, pricing, productising a service, prompting |

Use the **pack key** whenever a tool asks for the employee. If the job fits none of
them, leave the owner empty — the reviewer files it for every employee — and say so.

## Handoff

To Module 4 with the folder, the slug, the title and the owner's pack key (or none).
